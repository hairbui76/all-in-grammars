# Adjective Plus For

> [!abstract] In one line
> `For` follows adjectives that name a purpose, a suitability, or the person who benefits or suffers.

## Form

`be + adjective + for + noun / pronoun / V-ing`

## Suitability and purpose

| Combination | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| available **for** sth | ready to be used for | The hall is **available for** meetings. |
| convenient **for** | suiting the needs of | Is Monday **convenient for** you? |
| fit **for** | good enough for | The water is not **fit for** drinking. |
| suitable **for** | appropriate to | The film is not **suitable for** children. |
| perfect **for** | ideally matched to | This spot is **perfect for** a picnic. |
| qualified **for** | having the right training for | She is **qualified for** the post. |
| ready **for** sth | prepared for | Are you **ready for** the exam? |
| prepared **for** | made ready for | We were not **prepared for** the news. |
| necessary **for** | needed for | Sleep is **necessary for** recovery. |
| helpful / useful **for** | of benefit to | Maps are **useful for** hikers. |

## Effect on a person

| Combination | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| good **for** | beneficial to | Fruit is **good for** you. |
| bad **for** | harmful to | Stress is **bad for** the heart. |
| dangerous **for** | unsafe for | The road is **dangerous for** cyclists. |
| difficult **for** | hard for | Maths is **difficult for** him. |
| late **for** | arriving after the time of | I was **late for** class. |
| anxious **for** | worried about, eager for | She is **anxious for** news of her son. |

## Cause and responsibility

| Combination | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| famous **for** | well known because of | Paris is **famous for** its museums. |
| well-known **for** | widely known because of | He is **well-known for** his generosity. |
| greedy **for** | wanting too much of | The company was **greedy for** profit. |
| grateful **for** sth | thankful because of | I am **grateful for** your help. |
| sorry **for** | regretting, feeling pity for | I am **sorry for** what I said. |
| responsible **for** sth | in charge of, to blame for | He is **responsible for** the budget. |
| liable **for** sth | legally answerable for | The driver is **liable for** the damage. |

## Notes & exceptions

- `anxious for` and `anxious about` are both possible: `anxious about` a danger, `anxious for` a result you want.
- `good for` = beneficial, but `good at` = skilled → see [[Adjectives With Two Prepositions]].
- `responsible for` names the task; `responsible to` names the person you answer to.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ He is famous of his novels. → ✅ He is **famous for** his novels.
- ❌ I am sorry about you. → ✅ I am **sorry for** you.
