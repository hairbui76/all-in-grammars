# Adjective Plus In From And On

> [!abstract] In one line
> Three smaller sets of adjective + preposition collocations: `in` for involvement, `from` for separation or origin, `on` for reliance.

## Adjective + in

`be + adjective + in + noun / V-ing`

| Combination | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| absorbed **in** | deeply engaged in | He was **absorbed in** his book. |
| involved **in** | taking part in | She is **involved in** the project. |
| interested **in** | curious about, keen on | I am **interested in** archaeology. |
| rich **in** | containing a lot of | Oranges are **rich in** vitamin C. |
| successful **in** | doing well in | They were **successful in** the negotiation. |
| confident **in** sb / sth | trusting | We are **confident in** our team. |

## Adjective + from

`be + adjective + from + noun`

| Combination | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| absent **from** | not present at | He was **absent from** school. |
| different **from** | unlike | Your copy is **different from** mine. |
| safe **from** | protected against | The village is **safe from** floods. |
| isolated **from** | cut off from | The island was **isolated from** the mainland. |
| divorced **from** | legally separated from | She is **divorced from** her first husband. |
| descended **from** | coming by birth from | He is **descended from** a Scottish family. |
| far **from** | a long way from | The hotel is **far from** the station. |

## Adjective + on

`be + adjective + on + noun / V-ing`

| Combination | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| keen **on** | enthusiastic about | She is **keen on** swimming. |
| dependent **on** | relying on | The crop is **dependent on** rainfall. |

## Notes & exceptions

- `confident **in** sb` = you trust the person; `confident **of** sth` = you are sure it will happen → see [[Adjective Plus Of]].
- `dependent **on**` but `independent **of**`.
- `different from` is standard; `different to` (British) and `different than` (American) also occur.
- After all three prepositions a verb takes `-ing`: `keen **on** swimming`, `successful **in** finding` → see [[Gerund After Prepositions]].

## Common mistakes

- ❌ He is interested on history. → ✅ He is **interested in** history.
- ❌ It is different with mine. → ✅ It is **different from** mine.
