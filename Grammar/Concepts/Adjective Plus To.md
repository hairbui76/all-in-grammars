# Adjective Plus To

> [!abstract] In one line
> Adjectives that point at a person affected, or at something compared with, normally take the preposition `to`.

## Form

`be + adjective + to + noun / pronoun / V-ing`

## Adjectives describing an effect on someone

| Combination | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| available **to** sb | able to be used by | The library is **available to** all students. |
| delightful **to** sb | pleasing for | The music was **delightful to** the audience. |
| familiar **to** sb | already known by | The name is **familiar to** me. |
| clear **to** sb | easy to understand for | It was **clear to** everyone what he meant. |
| grateful **to** sb | thankful towards | I am **grateful to** you. |
| harmful **to** sb | damaging for | Smoking is **harmful to** your health. |
| important **to** sb | mattering to | Her opinion is **important to** me. |
| kind **to** sb | behaving well towards | Be **kind to** animals. |
| rude **to** sb | impolite towards | He was **rude to** the waiter. |
| useful **to** sb | of use for | This map is **useful to** travellers. |
| pleasant **to** sb | agreeable for | The weather was **pleasant to** us all. |
| responsible **to** sb | answerable to | Managers are **responsible to** the director. |
| necessary **to** sth / sb | needed for | Water is **necessary to** life. |
| open **to** sb | accessible to | The garden is **open to** the public. |
| profitable **to** sb | bringing gain to | The deal was **profitable to** both sides. |

## Adjectives of comparison, relation and attitude

| Combination | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| equal **to** | the same in amount as | One mile is **equal to** 1.6 kilometres. |
| identical **to** | exactly the same as | Her answer was **identical to** mine. |
| similar **to** | like | This case is **similar to** the last one. |
| contrary **to** | opposite to | **Contrary to** expectations, he won. |
| preferable **to** | better than | Walking is **preferable to** driving. |
| next **to** | beside | She sat **next to** me. |
| exposed **to** | left open to | The plants were **exposed to** frost. |
| favourable **to** | supporting, in favour of | The report was **favourable to** the plan. |
| accustomed **to** | in the habit of | He is **accustomed to** getting up early. |
| addicted **to** | unable to give up | She is **addicted to** her phone. |
| acceptable **to** | able to be accepted by | The terms were **acceptable to** the union. |
| agreeable **to** | willing to accept | They were **agreeable to** the proposal. |

## Notes & exceptions

- `able to`, `likely to`, `lucky to`, `liable to` and `willing to` are followed by a **bare infinitive**, not a noun: `He is **likely to** come.` → see [[Infinitives]].
- `accustomed to` and `addicted to` take `V-ing`, because here `to` is a preposition → see [[To As Preposition]].
- `liable to do smth` = likely to do it; `liable for smth` = legally answerable for it.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ She is accustomed to get up early. → ✅ She is **accustomed to getting** up early.
- ❌ It is similar with mine. → ✅ It is **similar to** mine.
