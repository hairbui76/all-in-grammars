# Adjective Plus With

> [!abstract] In one line
> `With` follows adjectives that describe a feeling directed at a **person**, or a relation of company, contents and likeness.

## Form

`be + adjective + with + noun / pronoun / V-ing`

## Feelings towards a person

| Combination | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| angry **with** sb | annoyed at someone | She was **angry with** her brother. |
| annoyed **with** sb | irritated by someone | He was **annoyed with** me. |
| furious **with** sb | extremely angry with | They were **furious with** the driver. |
| disappointed **with** sb | let down by | I was **disappointed with** him. |
| delighted **with** | very pleased about | We were **delighted with** the result. |
| pleased **with** | satisfied about | She was **pleased with** her marks. |
| satisfied **with** | content with | He is **satisfied with** the offer. |
| bored **with** | tired of | I am **bored with** this game. |
| fed up **with** | thoroughly tired of | They are **fed up with** the delays. |
| friendly **with** sb | on close terms with | He is **friendly with** the neighbours. |
| popular **with** sb | liked by | The teacher is **popular with** students. |

## Company, contents and likeness

| Combination | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| acquainted **with** sb | knowing someone slightly | I am **acquainted with** her father. |
| familiar **with** | having knowledge of | Are you **familiar with** this software? |
| busy **with** | occupied by | She is **busy with** her thesis. |
| crowded **with** | full of people or things | The street was **crowded with** shoppers. |
| concerned **with** | connected to, dealing with | The report is **concerned with** safety. |
| contrasted **with** | set against, compared to | His calm was **contrasted with** her panic. |
| compared **with** | measured against | Prices are low **compared with** last year. |
| identical **with** sth | exactly the same as | The copy is **identical with** the original. |

## Notes & exceptions

- Use `with` for a person and `at / about` for a thing: `angry **with** John` but `angry **at** the delay` → see [[Adjective Plus At]].
- `familiar with` = you know it; `familiar to` = it is known by someone → see [[Adjective Plus To]].
- `concerned with` = connected to a subject; `concerned about` = worried → see [[Adjective Plus About]].
- `identical with` and `identical to` are both correct.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ I am angry to you. → ✅ I am **angry with** you.
- ❌ She is bored of her job. → ✅ She is **bored with** her job.
