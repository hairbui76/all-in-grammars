# Adjective Suffixes

> [!abstract] In one line
> These endings are added to nouns and verbs to make adjectives describing a quality or characteristic.

## Form

`noun / verb + adjective suffix` → adjective

The endings are: `-able, -ous, -y, -ful, -ern, -ly, -al, -ish, -like, -ive, -ic, -ical` and the negative `-less`.

## Positive and descriptive endings

| Root | Adjective | Root | Adjective |
|---|---|---|---|
| change | changeable | poison | poisonous |
| rain | rainy | success | successful |
| north | northern | day | daily |
| music | musical | girl | girlish |
| child | childlike | attract | attractive |
| climate | climatic | alphabet | alphabetical |

- English weather is very **changeable**.
- Keep away from this chemical. It's very **poisonous**.
- He will be **successful** in his new job.
- Thanh Niên is a **daily** newspaper.
- The man has a **childlike** smile.
- She still wears rather young, **girlish** fashions.

## The negative ending -less

`noun + -less` → adjective meaning *without ...*

| Noun | Adjective |
|---|---|
| tree | treeless |
| child | childless |
| care | careless |
| rest | restless |
| price | priceless |
| harm | harmless |

- No trees can grow on that soil. It is **treeless**.
- Mr and Mrs Brown have no children. They're a **childless** couple.
- He failed because of his **careless** mistakes in his exam.
- My little brother was ill all night. My parents spent a **restless** night.
- Two of the paintings in the museum are **priceless**.
- Some people still think that smoking is **harmless**.

## Notes & exceptions

- `priceless` does **not** mean *without a price* — it means *too valuable to price*. Compare `worthless` = of no value.
- `-ic` and `-ical` can both exist on one root with different meanings: `historic` (of historical importance) vs `historical` (relating to history), `economic` vs `economical`.
- `-ly` on a noun makes an adjective (*daily*, *friendly*); on an adjective it usually makes an adverb (*quickly*).
