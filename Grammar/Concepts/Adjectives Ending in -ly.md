# Adjectives Ending in -ly

> [!abstract] In one line
> A small group of words end in `-ly` but are adjectives, not adverbs — they have no adverb form at all, so the idea must be expressed another way.

## The list

`friendly`, `lively`, `lonely`, `lovely`, `ugly`, `likely`, `silly`

(Also: `costly`, `cowardly`, `deadly`, `elderly`, `orderly`.)

## Examples

Used as ordinary adjectives:

- Our neighbours are **friendly**.
- I often feel **lonely** when I'm away from home.
- Your bedroom looks very **lovely**.
- It's a **silly** thing to say so.

## Notes & exceptions

- There is no adverb ~~friendlily~~ or ~~sillily~~. To modify a verb, use a prepositional phrase instead:

```
in a + adjective + way / manner
```

- She spoke **in a friendly way**.
- He acted **in a silly manner**.
- She greeted us **in a lovely manner**.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ She spoke to me friendly. → ✅ She spoke to me **in a friendly way**.
- ❌ He behaved sillily. → ✅ He behaved **in a silly manner**.
