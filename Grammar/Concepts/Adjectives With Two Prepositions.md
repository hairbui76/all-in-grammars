# Adjectives With Two Prepositions

> [!abstract] In one line
> Some adjectives take different prepositions with different meanings, and a few take two prepositions at once — one for the person, one for the thing.

## Same adjective, different preposition, different meaning

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| be tired **of** sth | be bored with it | I'm **tired of** doing the same work every day. |
| be tired **from** sth | be physically worn out by it | I'm **tired from** walking for a long time. |
| be good / bad **for** sb | beneficial / harmful | Exercise is **good for** you. |
| be good / bad **at** sth | skilled / unskilled in | She is **good at** drawing. |
| be kind / nice **to** sb | behave well towards | He was **kind to** the new pupil. |
| be angry **at** sth / **with** sb | annoyed by a thing / a person | I was **angry at** the delay but not **angry with** you. |
| be confident **of** sth / **in** sb | sure of an outcome / trusting a person | We are **confident of** success and **confident in** our team. |

## Two prepositions in one pattern

`be + adjective + to + person + for + thing`

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| be grateful **to** sb **for** sth | be thankful to someone because of something | I'm **grateful to** you **for** your help. |
| be responsible **to** sb **for** sth | answer to someone for a duty | He is **responsible to** the board **for** the budget. |

## It is + adjective + of / for + somebody

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| It's kind / nice **of** sb (to do sth) | judges the person's character | It's **kind of** you to say so. |
| It's difficult / easy **for** sb (to do sth) | judges the task, not the person | It's **difficult for** me to decide. |

## Notes & exceptions

- After a preposition an English verb takes the `-ing` form, never the infinitive → see [[Gerund After Prepositions]].
- The `of` / `for` split above is the same one used in [[It Is Adjective Of Somebody To Do]] and [[It Is Adjective For Somebody To Do]].

## Common mistakes

- ❌ I'm tired of walking so far, my legs hurt. → ✅ I'm **tired from** walking so far.
- ❌ It's kind for you to help. → ✅ It's **kind of** you to help.
