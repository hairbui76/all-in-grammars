# Asking For and Giving Information

> [!abstract] In one line
> Enquiring about prices, times, functions and facts — and matching the answer to the question word that was used.

## Useful patterns

| Asking about | Question | Typical answer |
|---|---|---|
| Price / cost | `What does it cost to get to ...?` · `How much is it?` | `It depends on how you go.` |
| Opening hours | `How late will the bank be open?` | `It will be open until 6 pm.` |
| Function | `What does this thing do?` | `It peels potatoes.` |
| Quantity / habit | `Do you smoke?` | `Yes, about ten cigarettes a day.` |
| Job | `What do you do for a living?` | `I work in a bank.` |
| Prediction | `Will the maths teacher give us a test this week?` | `No, he probably won't.` |
| Making an enquiry | `Hi, I'd like some information about ...` | `Sure, could I have your name and address?` |
| Booking | `Can I have an appointment with Dr Adams?` | `Yes, of course. When would suit you?` |

## When to use

- Match the answer to the question word: `What ... cost?` → an amount or `It depends ...`; `How late ...?` → a time; `What does it do?` → a function.
- `It depends on ...` is the standard answer when there is no single fact to give.
- Open a phone or counter enquiry with `I'd like some information about ...`, then expect to be asked for your details.

## Examples

- "**What does this thing do?**" — "**It peels potatoes.**"
- "**How late will the bank be open?**" — "**It will be open until 6 pm.**"
- "What does it cost to get to Manchester?" — "**It depends on how you go.**"
- "Hi, I'd like some information about driving lessons." — "**Sure, could I have your name and address?**"

## Common mistakes

- ❌ "How late will the bank be open?" — "It is two blocks away." (answers *where*, not *when*)
- ❌ "What does this thing do?" — "I think it's a waste of money." (an opinion, not a function)
