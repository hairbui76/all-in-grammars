# Comparative and Superlative

> [!abstract] In one line
> Short adjectives take `-er / -est`; long ones take `more / most`; a small set is irregular.

## Form

| Type | Comparative | Superlative |
|---|---|---|
| 1 syllable | `tall → taller` | `the tallest` |
| 1 syllable, CVC | `big → bigger` | `the biggest` |
| 2 syllables in `-y` | `happy → happier` | `the happiest` |
| other 2+ syllables | `modern → more modern` | `the most modern` |
| adverbs in `-ly` | `quickly → more quickly` | `the most quickly` |

`S + V + adj-er / more + adj + than + N`
`S + V + the + adj-est / the most + adj + (in/of ...)`

**Equality:** `as + adj/adv + as` · negative: `not as/so + adj + as`

### Irregular forms

| Adjective/Adverb | Comparative | Superlative |
|---|---|---|
| good / well | better | the best |
| bad / badly | worse | the worst |
| much, many | more | the most |
| little | less | the least |
| far | farther / further | the farthest / the furthest |
| old | older / elder | the oldest / the eldest |

## When to use

- **Comparative + than** for two items; **superlative + the** for three or more.
- `in` before a place or group, `of` before a plural or a period: *the tallest **in** the class*, *the best **of** the three*.
- **Double comparative** for gradual change: `adj-er and adj-er` / `more and more + adj`.
- **Parallel comparative** for proportional change: `The + comparative ..., the + comparative ...`.
- **Multiple comparison**: `twice / three times / half as + adj + as`.

## Examples

- This exam is **more difficult than** the last one.
- She is **the best** student **in** the school.
- He isn't **as tall as** his brother.
- The weather is getting **hotter and hotter**.
- **The harder** you work, **the more** you achieve.
- My flat is **twice as big as** yours.

## Notes & exceptions

- Never combine both markers: ❌ *more taller*, ❌ *the most biggest*.
- `elder / eldest` only before a noun and only for family members.
- `farther` = physical distance; `further` = additional (*further information*).
- After `than` a subject pronoun is formal (*than I am*), an object pronoun informal (*than me*).
- `less / the least` are the downward equivalents: *less expensive than*, *the least expensive*.
- Superlatives are often followed by [[Present Perfect]]: *the best film I **have ever seen***.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ She is more taller than me. → ✅ She is **taller** than me.
- ❌ He is the most tall in his class. → ✅ He is **the tallest** in his class.
- ❌ It is not so expensive than that one. → ✅ It is not so expensive **as** that one.
