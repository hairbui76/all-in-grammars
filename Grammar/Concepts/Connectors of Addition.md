# Connectors of Addition

> [!abstract] In one line
> Connectors of the `moreover` group add a further idea that supports the one already stated.

## The group

| Connector | Note |
|---|---|
| `moreover` | formal, adds a stronger supporting point |
| `furthermore` | formal, same function as `moreover` |
| `in addition` | neutral, written style |
| `also` | neutral, very common |
| `besides` | adds a decisive extra reason, often in speech |
| `similarly` | adds a point that is parallel, not merely extra |

## Examples

- Old cars tend to use more petrol. **Also**, there is a greater risk of accidents.
- I don't have time to see the film. **Besides**, it's had dreadful reviews.
- He got US dollars for the journey. **In addition**, he got travellers' cheques.
- Mr Brown has had years of experience dealing with student problems. **Furthermore**, he is sometimes confronted by a problem that he cannot handle by himself.

## Notes & exceptions

- `besides` is also a preposition meaning "apart from" (`besides the money`); as a connector it stands alone before a whole clause.
- `similarly` adds a comparable case rather than simply one more fact.
- Punctuation follows the general rule → see [[Sentence Connector Punctuation]].

## Contrast with

- [[Connectors of Contrast]] — the added idea opposes instead of supporting
- [[Connectors of Result]] — the second idea is a consequence, not an addition
- [[Inversion with Not Only But Also]] — addition inside one sentence
