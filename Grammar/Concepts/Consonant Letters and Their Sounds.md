# Consonant Letters and Their Sounds

> [!abstract] In one line
> Several consonant letters and letter groups have more than one pronunciation, usually decided by the letter that follows.

## Single letters

| Letter | Sounds | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `c` | `/k/` · `/s/` before `i, e, y` · `/ʃ/` | **c**an, **c**lose · **c**ity, **c**ertify, **c**ycle · spe**c**ial, o**c**ean |
| `d` | `/d/` · `/dʒ/` | **d**ay, **d**emand · e**d**ucate, gra**d**uate |
| `g` | `/g/` · `/dʒ/` before `i, e, y` · `/ʒ/` | **g**ain, re**g**ular · ori**g**in, **g**erm, **g**ymnast · **g**arage, bei**g**e |
| `n` | `/n/` · `/ŋ/` | **n**ot, fu**n** · u**n**cle, dri**n**k |
| `s` | `/s/` · `/z/` · `/ʃ/` · `/ʒ/` | **s**on, economi**s**t · ri**s**e, re**s**umption · **s**ure, **s**ugar · u**s**ually, occa**s**ion |
| `t` | `/t/` · `/tʃ/` · `/ʃ/` | **t**en, pre**t**end · cul**t**ure, ques**t**ion · men**t**ion, ini**t**ial |
| `x` | `/gz/` · `/ks/` · `/kʃ/` | e**x**ample, e**x**ist · bo**x**, mi**x**ture · lu**x**ury, an**x**ious |

## Consonant groups

| Group | Usual sound | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `-ch-` | `/k/` · `/tʃ/` · `/ʃ/` | **ch**emist, me**ch**anic · **ch**air, lun**ch** · ma**ch**ine, **ch**ef |
| `-gh-` / `-ph-` | `/f/` | rou**gh**, lau**gh**, **ph**otograph, paragra**ph** |
| `-qu-` | `/kw/` | **qu**een, re**qu**ire |
| `-sh-` | `/ʃ/` | **sh**are, sma**sh** |
| `-th-` | `/θ/` · `/ð/` | **th**ink, dep**th** · **th**em, wi**th**out |
