# Continuous Infinitive

> [!abstract] In one line
> `to be + V-ing` describes an action in progress at the moment being spoken about.

## Form

`(to) be + V-ing` — to be doing, to be working

## Uses

| After | Meaning | Equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| `will / shall` | future continuous | This time tomorrow, I **will be doing** my homework. |
| `must` | certain that it is happening now | `= I'm sure + present continuous clause` |
| `can't` | certain that it is not happening now | `= I'm sure + negative present continuous clause` |
| `may / might` | possibly happening now | `= Perhaps + present continuous clause` |
| `should` | it ought to be happening but isn't | criticism |
| `be said / thought to` | passive reporting | `People say that he is ...` |
| `seem, appear, pretend, happen` | apparent ongoing action | `It seems that he is ...` |
| `hope, promise, determine, arrange` | future ongoing action | |

## Examples

- Don't phone me at 12 o'clock tonight. I **will be sleeping** then.
- The house is quiet. Everybody **must be sleeping**. = I'm sure everybody is sleeping.
- It's noisy next door. My neighbours **must be having** a party.
- It's Sunday. They **can't be working** in the office. = I'm sure they are not working in the office.
- He looks serious. He **can't be joking**.
- They **may be hiding** in the wood. = Perhaps they are hiding in the wood.
- It is very cold. Mr Brown, who hasn't been well recently, is walking along the street without a coat. He **should be wearing** a warm coat.
- You **shouldn't be talking**. You **should be concentrating** on the lesson.
- People say that he is studying abroad. → He **is said to be studying** abroad.
- He **seems to be running** away from your fierce dog. = It looks as if he is running away.
- I happened **to be standing** next to the child when he fell.
- She pretends / pretended **to be sleeping**.
- I hope **to be studying** at a university this time next year.
- I promise **to be waiting** for you at the cinema gate at 7 o'clock tonight.
