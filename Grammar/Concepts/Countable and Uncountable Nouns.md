# Countable and Uncountable Nouns

> [!abstract] In one line
> Countable nouns have a plural and take `a/an` and numbers; uncountable nouns do not.

## Form

| | Countable | Uncountable |
|---|---|---|
| Singular article | `a book`, `an apple` | no `a/an` |
| Plural | `books`, `apples` | no plural `-s` |
| Numbers | `three chairs` | `three pieces of advice` |
| Quantifiers | `many`, `few`, `a few`, `a number of` | `much`, `little`, `a little`, `an amount of` |
| Both | `some`, `any`, `a lot of`, `plenty of`, `no`, `the` | same |
| Verb | singular or plural | always **singular** |

## When to use

- Uncountable nouns cover **substances** (water, rice, gold), **abstract ideas** (information, advice, knowledge, progress), **activities** (homework, work, research), and **collectives** (furniture, luggage, equipment, money).
- To count an uncountable noun, use a **partitive**: `a piece of advice`, `a bar of soap`, `a loaf of bread`, `two glasses of water`, `an item of news`.

## Examples

- She gave me some useful **advice**. (not *advices*)
- **Furniture** is expensive here. (singular verb)
- I need two **pieces of information** before I can decide.
- How **much luggage** are you taking? / How **many bags** are you taking?
- There **is** a lot of **traffic** today.

## Notes & exceptions

- Frequently mistaken uncountables: `advice, information, news, furniture, luggage, baggage, equipment, homework, work, progress, research, knowledge, money, traffic, weather, accommodation, machinery`.
- `news` ends in `-s` but is uncountable and singular: *The news **is** good.*
- Some nouns change meaning with countability:
  - `a paper` = newspaper / essay · `paper` = material
  - `a hair` = one strand · `hair` = all of it
  - `an experience` = an event · `experience` = knowledge gained
  - `a room` = a chamber · `room` = space
  - `an iron` = an appliance · `iron` = metal
- Uncountable nouns take a zero article for general meaning and `the` when specific.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ many informations → ✅ **much information** / **a lot of information**
- ❌ She has a long hairs. → ✅ She has long **hair**.
- ❌ The furnitures are new. → ✅ The **furniture is** new.
