# English Vowel Sounds

> [!abstract] In one line
> English has 20 vowel sounds; twelve of them are single (pure) vowels written with one IPA symbol.

## The single vowels

| Symbol | Length / quality | Example words |
|---|---|---|
| `/ɪ/` | short i | l**i**ve, **i**t, s**i**t, th**i**s, **i**s |
| `/iː/` | long i | l**ea**ve, s**ea**t, m**ea**t, sp**ea**k |
| `/ɒ/` | short o | n**o**t, p**o**t, h**o**t, **o**n |
| `/ɔː/` | long o | g**o**ne, d**au**ghter, fl**oo**r, d**oo**r |
| `/æ/` | between a and e | **a**t, m**a**n, m**a**p, h**a**t, m**a**d |
| `/ɑː/` | long a | st**a**r, c**a**r, f**a**r |
| `/e/` | e | r**e**d, b**e**d, m**e**n, thr**ea**ten, inst**ea**d |
| `/ʌ/` | short, central | m**u**st, b**u**t, h**u**t, bl**oo**d, c**u**t |
| `/ɜː/` | long, central | s**ir**, b**ir**d, l**ear**n |
| `/ə/` | schwa, unstressed | **a**, **a**bout, **a**round, **a**fraid, **a**live |
| `/ʊ/` | short u | l**oo**k, g**oo**d, p**u**t, b**oo**k, c**oo**k |
| `/uː/` | long u | m**oo**n, f**oo**d, r**oo**m, s**oo**n, n**oo**n, t**oo** |

## Notes & exceptions

- The count of 20 vowels includes the diphthongs — see [[English Diphthongs and Triphthongs]].
- `/ə/` only occurs in unstressed syllables, so it is decided by stress, not by spelling — see [[Word Stress]].
- The same letter can stand for several of these sounds; the spelling-to-sound mapping is in [[Vowel Letters and Their Sounds]].
