# Expressions With Do

> [!abstract] In one line
> `Do` is used for performing an action, activity, task or job, and for vague general activity.

## When to use

1. **Performing an action, activity or task**
   `do a crossword`, `do the ironing`, `do the laundry`, `do the washing`, `do the washing up`
2. **Work of any kind**
   `do your work`, `do homework`, `do housework`, `do your job`
3. **General ideas** — when you describe an action without saying exactly what it is. Very common with `something, nothing, anything, everything`.

## Examples

- I'm not **doing anything** today.
- He **does everything** for his mother.
- She's **doing nothing**.
- You can watch TV after you've **done your homework**.
- I don't like being criticized after I've **done my best**.

## Common fixed expressions

| Expression | Expression | Expression |
|---|---|---|
| `do badly` | `do business` | `do the dishes` |
| `do a favour` | `do harm` | `do well` |
| `do your best` | `do your hair` | `do your nails` |
| `do your worst` | `do research` | `do your duty` |

## Common mistakes

- ❌ make the washing up → ✅ **do** the washing up
- ❌ make your homework → ✅ **do** your homework
- ❌ do a mistake → ✅ **make** a mistake
