# First Conditional

> [!abstract] In one line
> A real, possible condition in the present or future, with its likely result.

## Form

`If + S + V(present simple), S + will/shall + V(bare)`

The if-clause never takes `will`; the future meaning is carried by the main clause.

## When to use

- A condition that may well happen now or in the future
- Predictions, promises, warnings, threats
- Scientific or general facts (with present simple in both clauses)

## Examples

- If you **work** hard, you **will pass** the exam.
- If it **is** fine, I **shall go** for a swim.
- What **will happen** if nuclear tests **do not stop**?
- If I **pass** the university entrance exam, my parents **will give** me a new motorbike.
- Crime rate **will increase** if the government **does not implement** this regulation.

## Notes & exceptions

**Variations in the if-clause**

- Present continuous for an action in progress: *If you **are teaching**, I will phone you later.* / *If they **are staying** over night, I will book a room in advance.*
- Present perfect for a completed action with no stated time: *If you **haven't eaten** anything, I'll cook something for you.* / *If it **has stopped** raining, we will set off.*
- `will` in the if-clause is possible in polite requests, where it means "be willing to": *If you **will come** this way, I'll show you your room.*

**Variations in the main clause**

- `may/might` for a possible result: *If you don't take a map with you, you **may get** lost.*
- `can` for permission or ability: *If you have finished your work, you **can go** home.*
- `must` for obligation: *If you want to pass the exam, you **must work** harder.*
- `should / had better` for advice: *If you want to keep fit, you **should** take exercise regularly.*
- Imperative for an order: *If you are hungry, **eat** something.*
- Present simple for an inevitable, law-like result: *If you **heat** ice, it **turns** to water.* / *If it rains, the grass **gets** wet.*

## Common mistakes

- ❌ If it will rain, we will stay in. → ✅ If it **rains**, we will stay in.
