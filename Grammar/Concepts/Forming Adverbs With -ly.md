# Forming Adverbs With -ly

> [!abstract] In one line
> Most adverbs of manner and degree are made by adding `-ly` to the corresponding adjective, with a few predictable spelling changes.

## Form

```
Adjective + ly = Adverb
```

- quick → **quickly**
- beautiful → **beautifully**
- slow → **slowly**
- grave → **gravely**
- immediate → **immediately**

## Spelling rules

| Adjective ending | Change | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| consonant + `-y` | `y` → `i` + `ly` | easy → **easily**, happy → **happily** |
| `-ble` / `-ible` | drop `e`, add `y` | possible → **possibly**, capable → **capably**, sensible → **sensibly** |
| `-ic` | add `-ally` | automatic → **automatically** |
| final `-e` | normally keep the `e` | extreme → **extremely** |
| vowel + `l` | just add `-ly` | beautiful → **beautifully**, final → **finally** |

## Examples

- He was driving **carefully**.
- My brother did his work **successfully**.
- The girl sings **beautifully**.
- He drives **slowly**.

## Notes & exceptions

- Irregular: `true` → **truly**, `due` → **duly**, `whole` → **wholly** (the final `e` is dropped).
- `good` has the irregular adverb **well** → see [[Good and Well]].
- Some `-ly` words are adjectives, not adverbs (`friendly`, `lovely`, `silly`) → see [[Adjectives Ending in -ly]].
- Some adverbs have the same form as the adjective and take no `-ly` (`fast`, `hard`, `late`) → see [[Adjectives and Adverbs With the Same Form]].

## Common mistakes

- ❌ a slowly driver → ✅ He is a **slow** driver. / He drives **slowly**.
- ❌ He works goodly. → ✅ He works **well**.
