# Future Simple

> [!abstract] In one line
> `will + bare infinitive` — a decision, prediction, promise or offer made about the future.

## Form

`(+) S + will + V(bare)`
`(-) S + will not (won't) + V(bare)`
`(?) Will + S + V(bare) ?`

`shall` is possible with `I/we`, mainly in formal English and in offers/suggestions (`Shall I help you?`).

## When to use

- A **spontaneous decision** taken at the moment of speaking.
- A **prediction** or opinion about the future, especially after `think`, `believe`, `expect`, `be sure`, `probably`, `perhaps`.
- **Promises, offers, requests, threats, refusals** (`won't` = refuses to).
- In the main clause of the [[First Conditional]] and after time clauses → see [[Clauses of Time]].

## Examples

- The phone is ringing — I **will get** it.
- I think it **will rain** this evening.
- I **will help** you with your homework, I promise.
- **Will** you **open** the window, please?
- If you study hard, you **will pass** the exam.

## Notes & exceptions

- **Never use `will` in a time clause or if-clause**: after `when`, `as soon as`, `until`, `before`, `after`, `if`, use a present tense.
  - *I will call you when I **arrive**.* (not *will arrive*)
- Time markers: `tomorrow`, `next week`, `soon`, `in 2030`, `one day`, `in the future`.
- Passive: `will be + V3` → see [[Passive Voice in Nine Tenses]].
- In [[Reported Speech]] `will` becomes `would` → see [[Backshift of Tenses]].

## Common mistakes

- ❌ I will to go. → ✅ I **will go**.
- ❌ When he will come, we will start. → ✅ When he **comes**, we will start.
- ❌ Look at those clouds — it will rain. → ✅ Look at those clouds — it **is going to rain**. (present evidence)

## Contrast with

- [[Be Going To]] — a plan already decided, or a prediction based on present evidence
- [[Present Continuous]] — a fixed personal arrangement (*I'm meeting Sam at six.*)
- [[Present Simple]] — timetables and schedules (*The train leaves at 7.*)
- [[Future Continuous]] — in progress at a future moment
- [[Future Perfect]] — completed before a future moment
