# Greetings and Small Talk

> [!abstract] In one line
> Fixed openings, follow-up questions and closings that keep an everyday conversation going.

## Useful patterns

| Function | Typical opening | Typical response |
|---|---|---|
| Asking how someone is | `How have you been recently?` | `Pretty busy, I think.` |
| Asking about a shared situation | `So how are things at school, Tom?` | `Oh, pretty good, actually.` |
| Checking someone's state | `Are you feeling OK?` | `No, I'm not. I feel awful.` |
| Asking about the weekend | `Did you do anything special last weekend?` | `No, nothing special.` |
| Asking about work | `What do you do for a living?` | `I work in a bank.` |
| Asking about plans | `Where will you go on vacation?` | `Probably to the beach.` |
| Announcing news | `Guess what! I've just been invited to the manager's house for dinner.` | `Oh, how nice.` |
| Introducing news | `Did you hear about the robbery the other day?` | `No, what happened?` |
| Showing mild surprise | `We've been there before.` | `Oh, have we?` |
| Closing a conversation | `Well, it's been nice talking to you.` | `Yes, nice to have met you too.` |

## When to use

- Answer a `How ...?` question with a **manner or state** word (`Pretty busy`, `Pretty good`), not with a time or place.
- Answer a `Where ...?` question with a **place** (`Probably to the beach`), even when the answer is vague.
- When someone shares news, react before asking for detail: `Oh, how nice.` / `Really?` / `No, what happened?`

## Examples

- "How have you been recently?" — "**Pretty busy, I think.**"
- "Guess what! I've just been invited to the manager's house for dinner." — "**Oh, how nice.**"
- "Well, it's been nice talking to you." — "**Yes, nice to have met you too.**"

## Common mistakes

- ❌ "How have you been recently?" — "By bus, I think." (answers *how* as a means of transport)
- ❌ "Well, it's been nice talking to you." — "Nice to meet you, too." (the meeting is now over, so a present-tense greeting is wrong)

## Contrast with

- [[Thanking and Complimenting]] — reacting to a favour or praise rather than to news
- [[Asking For and Giving Opinions]] — asking what someone thinks rather than how they are
