# However and No Matter

> [!abstract] In one line
> `however` + adjective/adverb and `no matter` + wh-word introduce a concessive clause meaning "it makes no difference which / how much".

## Form

```
However + adj / adv + S + V, + Main clause
No matter + what / where / when / who / which + Clause, + Main clause
No matter how + adj / adv + S + V, + Main clause
It doesn't matter + wh-clause, + Main clause
It makes no difference + wh-clause
```

## Equivalences

| `no matter` form | one-word equivalent |
|---|---|
| no matter what | whatever |
| no matter who | whoever |
| no matter where | wherever |
| no matter when | whenever |
| no matter how | however |

## Examples

- **However carefully he drives**, he often meets with accidents.
- **However tired I am**, I am trying to work.
- **However long it may take**, I will wait for you to return.
- **No matter what** (= Whatever) I do, my parents find fault with it.
- **No matter who** (= Whoever) you are, I still love you.
- **No matter where** (= Wherever) we go, we will never forget each other.
- **No matter how** (= however) experienced you may be, driving fast is dangerous.
- **No matter how long** it may take, I will find that girl.
- **It doesn't matter where** you go, you must drive very carefully.
- **It doesn't matter to me which** film we go to see tonight.
- **It makes no difference to me what** we will have for dinner.
- **It doesn't matter to me who** I am going to get married to.

## Notes & exceptions

- `however` must be immediately followed by the adjective or adverb it modifies, then the subject and verb: `However tired I am, ...` — never `However I am tired`.
- `may / might` is often added in these clauses to stress the hypothetical nature: *No matter how experienced you **may** be, ...*.
- Do not confuse this `however` with the linking adverb `however` meaning "but", which is followed by a comma.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ However he drives carefully, he has accidents. → ✅ **However carefully he drives**, he has accidents.
- ❌ No matter how he is rich, he isn't happy. → ✅ **No matter how rich he is**, he isn't happy.
