# Impersonal Passive

> [!abstract] In one line
> Reporting verbs such as `say`, `think` and `believe` can be made passive in two ways: with an introductory `It is said that ...` or by raising the subject into `He is said to ...`.

## Form

Verbs used with these patterns: `say` · `believe` · `think` · `consider` · `report` ·
`know` · `expect` · `understand` · `allege` · `acknowledge` · `estimate` · `rumour` ·
`explain` · `assume` · `presume` · `suppose` · `announce`

**Pattern 1**

`It is/was + P2 + that + clause`

**Pattern 2**

`S + be + P2 + to + ...`, where the infinitive takes one of four shapes:

| Shape | Use it when the verb of the `that`-clause is in ... |
|---|---|
| `to + V` | present simple or future simple |
| `to be + V-ing` | present continuous or future continuous |
| `to have + P2` | present perfect or past simple |
| `to have been + V-ing` | present perfect continuous or future perfect continuous |

## Examples

Pattern 1:

- People say that he is 100 years old. → **It is said that** he is 100 years old.
- People expect that the strike will end soon. → **It is expected that** the strike will end soon.

Pattern 2, shape `to + V`:

- People say that he is 100 years old. → He **is said to be** 100 years old.
- People expect that the strike will end soon. → The strike **is expected to end** soon.
- Everybody says that there is a secret tunnel between those two houses. → **There is said to be** a secret tunnel between those two houses.
- People said that he was a dedicated worker. → He **was said to be** a dedicated worker.

Pattern 2, shape `to be + V-ing`:

- They think that the terrorists are hiding in the mountain. → The terrorists **are thought to be hiding** in the mountain.
- People believe that she is studying abroad. → She **is believed to be studying** abroad.

Pattern 2, shape `to have + P2`:

- People say that he escaped to a neutral country. → He **is said to have escaped** to a neutral country.
- People think that she has made progress in her studies. → She **is thought to have made** progress in her studies.
- People allege that he stole the money. → He **is alleged to have stolen** the money.

Pattern 2, shape `to have been + V-ing`:

- People say that he has been playing football since he was 5. → He **is said to have been playing** football since he was 5.

## Notes & exceptions

- Pattern 1 corresponds only to an active sentence whose subject is a general plural — `people`, `everybody`, `they` in the impersonal sense.
- The tense of the passive verb (`is said` / `was said`) follows the tense of the original reporting verb, while the infinitive shows the time of the reported event.
- `There is/are ...` in the reported clause is raised as `There is said to be ...`.
