# Must For Deduction

> [!abstract] In one line
> `Must` expresses a logical conclusion the speaker is certain about, as well as an obligation the speaker imposes on himself.

## Form

| Time reference | Structure | Equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| now, a state | `S + must + V` | `I'm sure + present simple clause` |
| now, in progress | `S + must be + V-ing` | `I'm sure + present continuous clause` |
| past, completed | `S + must have + PII` | `I'm sure + past simple clause` |
| past, in progress | `S + must have been + V-ing` | `I'm sure + past continuous clause` |

## Obligation use

`Must` also states a duty the speaker feels personally, not one imposed from outside.

- I have a lot of work to do tonight. I think I **must stay** up late.
- It's too late. I **must go** now.

## Examples — present deduction

- She hasn't eaten anything for two days. She **must be** hungry now.
- I smell something burning. There **must be** a fire nearby.
- He has been living in England since he was a child. He **must speak** English very well.
- She **must have** a lot of money in the bank. = I'm sure she has a lot of money in the bank.

## Examples — present continuous deduction

- The house is quiet. Everybody **must be sleeping**. = I'm sure everybody is sleeping.
- It's noisy next door. My neighbours **must be having** a party.
- The terrorists **must be hiding** in the mountain.
- My father **must be watching** TV at home now.

## Examples — past deduction

- Liverpool won the match. They **must have played** very well. = I'm sure they played very well.
- I've lost my key. I **must have dropped** it somewhere in this room.
- It **must have taken** the Ancient Egyptians years to build their pyramids.
- He **must have left** the library book on the bus.
- Vietnamese people defeated the American imperialists. Vietnamese leaders **must have been** intelligent.

## Examples — past continuous deduction

- It was noisy next door. My neighbours **must have been having** a party.
- My father **must have been working** in his office this time yesterday.

## Notes & exceptions

- The negative of deductive `must` is **not** `mustn't` but `can't` → see [[Cannot For Negative Deduction]].
