# Nouns Followed By Infinitive

> [!abstract] In one line
> A set of abstract nouns — mostly nouns derived from verbs of intention, effort and willingness — takes a full infinitive as its complement.

## Form

`(determiner +) noun + to + V`

## The list

ability · ambition · anxiety · attempt · decision · demand · desire · determination ·
eagerness · effort · failure · offer · plan · promise · refusal · request · scheme · willingness · wish

## Examples

- His **ability to get on** with people is his chief asset.
- He made an **attempt / effort to make ends meet**.
- **Failure to obey** the regulation may result in disqualification.
- Their **offer / promise / plan to rebuild** the town wasn't taken seriously.
- I was appreciated by your **willingness to help** me.

## Notes & exceptions

- Many of these nouns correspond to a verb that also takes the infinitive (`decide → decision`, `refuse → refusal`, `attempt → attempt`) → see [[Verbs Followed By Infinitive]].
- Do not confuse this with a noun + preposition + gerund pattern (`the difficulty of doing`, `no chance of winning`).

## Contrast with

- [[Gerunds]] — nouns followed by `preposition + V-ing`
