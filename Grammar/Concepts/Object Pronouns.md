# Object Pronouns

> [!abstract] In one line
> The form a personal pronoun takes when it is the target of an action, i.e. after a verb or a preposition.

## The set

| Subject | Object |
|---|---|
| I | me |
| you | you |
| he | him |
| she | her |
| it | it |
| we | us |
| you | you |
| they | them |

## When to use

- As the direct or indirect object of a verb
- After a preposition

## Examples

- I like **him**.
- I'd like to talk to **her**.
- He is stronger than **me**.

## Notes & exceptions

- `you` and `it` do not change form.
- In comparisons both forms are heard: *He is stronger than **me*** (informal) / *than **I** am* (formal) — see [[Comparative and Superlative]].
