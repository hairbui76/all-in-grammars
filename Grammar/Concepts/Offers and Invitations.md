# Offers and Invitations

> [!abstract] In one line
> Proposing to help someone, or inviting them to do something with you, and the fixed replies that go with each.

## Useful patterns

### Offering help or a service

| Register | Offer | Typical response |
|---|---|---|
| Service / shop | `May I help you?` | `Yes. Do you have any shirts?` |
| Neutral | `Do you want me to help you with ...?` | `No, I can manage them myself.` |
| Informal | `Let me + V ...` | `No problem.` / `Don't worry. I'm all right.` |
| Restaurant | `Would you like to order now?` | `Yes, I'd like beef steak.` |
| Restaurant | `Would you like anything else?` | `No, thank you. That'll be all.` |

### Inviting

| Register | Invitation | Typical response |
|---|---|---|
| Polite | `Would you like to ...?` | `Yes, I'd love to.` |
| Neutral | `Can you come over after ...?` | `That would be nice.` |
| Informal | `Do you fancy + V-ing ...?` | `That would be nice.` |
| Informal | `How about + V-ing / noun?` | `That sounds nice.` |
| Indirect hint | `There is a baseball game tonight.` | `Great. Let's go.` |
| Indirect hint | `I've got two tickets for the show.` | `That's great. When is it?` |

## When to use

- `Would you like ...?` is answered with `I'd like ...` or `I'd love to`, **not** with `I like ...` — `would like` is a wish for one occasion, `like` is a general preference.
- A statement can work as an indirect invitation (`I've got two tickets for the show.`). Reply with enthusiasm plus a follow-up question rather than with a bare comment.
- `Let me ...` offers help without asking permission, so the refusal is a reassurance (`Don't worry, I'm all right.`), not a `No`.

## Examples

- "Do you want me to help you with those suitcases?" — "**No, I can manage them myself.**"
- "Would you like to order now?" — "Yes, **I'd like** beef steak."
- "Do you fancy going to a movie this evening?" — "**That would be nice.**"
- "Let me drive you home." — "**Don't worry. I'm all right.**"

## Common mistakes

- ❌ "Would you like to order now?" — "Yes, I like beef steak."
- ❌ "How about an evening riverboat tour?" — "No, it's good to do so." (contradicts itself)
