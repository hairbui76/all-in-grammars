# Participle Clauses

> [!abstract] In one line
> A subordinate clause can be shortened by deleting its subject and conjunction and turning its verb into a participle.

## Form

```
Since / As / Because + S + V   →  V-ing ...            (reason)
When / While + S + V           →  When + V-ing ...     (time)
Because + S + do not + V       →  Not + V-ing ...      (negative reason)
```

Use `Being + noun/adjective` when the original verb is `be`, and `There being ...` when the clause begins with `there is / there was`.

## Reducing clauses of reason (`since`, `as`, `because`)

- **Since we had** nothing to do, we went to bed early. → **Having** nothing to do, we went to bed early.
- **As there was** too much fog, they had to delay all the flights. → **There being** too much fog, they had to delay all the flights.
- **There being** many people on the beach, I couldn't find him.
- **Because he didn't know** the language, he found it hard to communicate with people there. → **Not knowing** the language, he found it hard to communicate with people there.
- **Fearing** that he would be recognized, he didn't go out in the daylight.
- **Feeling** tired, I went out for a change.
- **Being** a pupil, I have to obey my school regulations.

## Reducing clauses with `when`

The conjunction `when` is usually kept:

- **When you buy** a motorbike, you should take petrol consumption into account. → **When buying** a motorbike, you should take petrol consumption into account.
- **When applying** for a job, you should take salary and working conditions into account.
- **When driving** a car, you should wear seatbelts.

## Notes & exceptions

- The negative particle goes **before** the participle: `Not knowing`, `Not having`.
- If the original verb is passive, use a past participle instead: `When used abroad, a credit card is very convenient.` → see [[Past Participle]].
- The subject of the participle should normally be the subject of the main clause; otherwise the sentence becomes a dangling participle.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ Knowing not the language, he found it hard to communicate. → ✅ **Not knowing** the language, ...
- ❌ Being too much fog, they delayed the flights. → ✅ **There being** too much fog, ...
