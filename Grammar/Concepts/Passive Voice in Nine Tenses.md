# Passive Voice in Nine Tenses

> [!abstract] In one line
> The passive is always `be + past participle`; only the form of `be` changes from tense to tense.

## Form

| Tense | Active | Passive |
|---|---|---|
| Present simple | `S + V(s/es) + O` | `S + am/is/are + P2 + by + Agent` |
| Present continuous | `S + am/is/are + V-ing + O` | `S + am/is/are + being + P2 + by + Agent` |
| Present perfect | `S + have/has + P2 + O` | `S + have/has + been + P2 + by + Agent` |
| Past simple | `S + V2 + O` | `S + was/were + P2 + by + Agent` |
| Past continuous | `S + was/were + V-ing + O` | `S + was/were + being + P2 + by + Agent` |
| Past perfect | `S + had + P2 + O` | `S + had + been + P2 + by + Agent` |
| Near future | `S + am/is/are + going to + V + O` | `S + am/is/are + going to be + P2 + by + Agent` |
| Simple future | `S + will/shall + V + O` | `S + will/shall + be + P2 + by + Agent` |
| Future perfect | `S + will/shall have + P2 + O` | `S + will/shall have been + P2 + by + Agent` |

## Examples

- They sweep the streets every night. → The streets **are swept** (by them) every night.
- We don't water the flowers in the morning. → The flowers **are not watered** in the morning.
- They are building a bridge over the river. → A bridge **is being built** over the river by them.
- He isn't decorating the room today. → The room **isn't being decorated**.
- The terrorist has kept three hostages for a week. → Three hostages **have been kept** by the terrorist for a week.
- The thieves broke into the house. → The house **was broken into** by the thieves.
- They were doing a physical experiment when I was there. → A physical experiment **was being done** by them when I was there.
- Nobody told me that they had discharged John from hospital. → I **was not told** that John **had been discharged** from hospital.
- We are going to make him the monitor of our class. → He **is going to be made** the monitor of our class by us.
- They will change the date of the meeting again. → The date of the meeting **will be changed** again by them.
- By the time you come back, they will have finished the project. → By the time you come back, the project **will have been finished**.

## Notes & exceptions

- Questions follow the same rule: `What do they call the new hotel?` → `What **is** the hotel **called**?`
- Phrasal verbs keep their particle in the passive: `break into` → `was broken into`.
- Present perfect continuous, past perfect continuous and future continuous are normally not used in the passive.
