# Passive of Make Help and Let

> [!abstract] In one line
> `make`, `help` and `let` take a bare infinitive in the active, but the passive requires `to + infinitive` (or a paraphrase with `be allowed to`).

## Form

| Active | Passive |
|---|---|
| `make sb do smth` | `be made to do smth` |
| `help sb (to) do smth` | `be helped to do smth` |
| `let sb do smth` | `be let do smth` / more usually `be allowed to do smth` |
| `let sb do smth` (subject = object) | `let oneself be done` |

## When to use

- Use `be allowed to` instead of `be let` whenever the passive subject is different from the active object — `be let` is awkward in most contexts.
- Use `let oneself be + P2` when the active subject and the object refer to the same person.

## Examples

- The teacher is making us **work** hard. → We are being made **to work** hard by our teacher.
- He helped me (to) **repair** the washing machine. → I was helped **to repair** the washing machine by him.
- His parents didn't let him buy a motorbike. → He **wasn't let buy** (**wasn't allowed to buy**) a motorbike by his parents.
- He let her cheat him. → He **let himself be cheated** by her.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ We are being made work hard. → ✅ We are being made **to** work hard.
- ❌ He was let to buy a motorbike. → ✅ He was **allowed to** buy a motorbike.
