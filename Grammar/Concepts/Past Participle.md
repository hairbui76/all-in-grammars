# Past Participle

> [!abstract] In one line
> The third form of the verb (`V-ed` or an irregular form), used with a passive or completed meaning.

## Form

```
V-ed / irregular past participle
```

Examples: `boiled, wanted, broken, spoken, written`.

## Main uses

1. **In the three perfect tenses** (present perfect, past perfect, future perfect and their variants)
2. **Before a noun, as an adjective** with a passive meaning: `V-ed/Irregular Verb + N`
3. **In the passive voice**
4. **As a reduced clause**, replacing a subject + passive verb, exactly as the present participle replaces a subject + active verb

## Examples

### Perfect tenses

- He thanked me for what I **had done** for him.
- I'm sorry for what I **have done**.
- By the end of this year, they **will have worked** here for 5 years.

### As an adjective

- **boiled** water, a **broken** leg, **fried** rice, **boiled** chicken, **grilled** lamb chops, **steamed** sticky rice

### In the passive

- I liked people praising my husband. → I liked my husband **being praised**.
- People say that he was executed in public. → He **is said to have been executed** in public. / It **is said** that he was executed in public.
- They couldn't find his passport anywhere. → His passport **was** nowhere **to be found**.
- I want him to paint the gate green. → I wanted the gate **to be painted** by him.
- What should we do to help the poor? → What **should be done** by us to help the poor?

### As a reduced clause

- The man **who has been arrested** by the police is a dangerous criminal. → The man **arrested** by the police is a dangerous criminal.
- Because the bridge **had been weakened** by the successive storms, it was no longer safe. → **Weakened by successive storms**, the bridge was no longer safe.
- **When used** abroad, a credit card is very convenient.
