# Perfect Continuous Infinitive

> [!abstract] In one line
> `to have been + V-ing` refers to an action that was in progress at, or lasted up to, an earlier point of time.

## Form

`(to) have been + V-ing` — to have been doing, to have been making

## Uses

| After | Formula | Equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| `will / shall` | `will have been V-ing` | future perfect continuous |
| `must` | `S + must have been V-ing` | `I'm sure + past continuous clause` |
| `can't / couldn't` | `S + can't have been V-ing` | `I'm sure + negative past continuous clause` |
| `may / might` | `S + may/might have been V-ing` | `Perhaps + past continuous clause` |
| `should` | `S + should have been V-ing` | it ought to have been happening but wasn't |
| `be said / thought to` | passive reporting | `People say that he has been ...` |
| `seem, appear, pretend, happen` | apparent long-running action | `It seems that she has been ...` |
| `hope, promise, determine, expect, arrange` | duration up to a future point | |

## Examples

- By the end of this year, I will **have been teaching** for 12 years.
- By this time next year, they will **have been working** in this factory for 5 years.
- It was noisy next door. My neighbours **must have been having** a party. = I'm sure my neighbours were having a party.
- The house was quiet when I returned. Everybody **must have been sleeping**.
- Jack walked straight into the wall. He **can't have been looking** where he was going.
- The children **couldn't have been studying** at school at 10 p.m. yesterday.
- They **might have been playing** sports this time yesterday. = Perhaps they were playing sports.
- When I arrived, he was watching TV. He **shouldn't have been watching** TV. He **should have been doing** his homework.
- People say that he has been playing football since he was a child. → He **is said to have been playing** football.
- He **is thought to have been working** for an international organization for a long time.
- She seems **to have been waiting** for you for a long time.
- I happened **to have been standing** there for a few minutes when the fire broke out.
- She pretends **to have been learning** English for a long time. In fact, she is a beginner.
- I hope **to have been teaching** English for a long time before I'm retired.

- [[Perfect Infinitive]] — a completed earlier action
- [[Continuous Infinitive]] — an action in progress now
