# Perfect Infinitive

> [!abstract] In one line
> `to have + past participle` places the action of the infinitive earlier than the action of the main verb.

## Form

`(to) have + PII` — to have done, to have made

## Uses

| After | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `will / shall` | future perfect |
| `was / were to` | a past plan that was not carried out |
| `must` | certainty about the past |
| `may / might` | possibility about the past |
| `can't / couldn't` | certainty that it did not happen |
| `should / had better / ought to` | past action that was advisable but not done |
| `needn't` | past action that was done but was unnecessary |
| `could` | past ability that was not used |
| `would like / would rather / would sooner` | an unreal wish about the past |
| `be said / thought to` | passive reporting of a past event |
| `seem, appear, pretend, happen` | apparent earlier action |
| `hope, promise, determine, arrange, expect` | an action to be completed by a future point |

## Examples

- By the end of this year, I will **have taught** 12 courses.
- Don't phone me at 4 o'clock tomorrow morning. I won't **have got up** by then.
- The house **was to have been** ready today. (but it isn't)
- The car **was to have been repaired**.
- The papers **were to have been given** back to us.
- She pretends **to have saved** a lot of money. = She pretends that she has saved a lot of money.
- I happen **to have met** him. = It happens that I have met him.
- They appear **to have lived** in this city for a long time.
- No one seems **to have predicted** the correct answer. = It seems that no one has predicted the correct answer.
- You seem / seemed **to have put** on weight.
- I hope **to have saved** enough money to buy a car by the end of this year.
- I promise **to have learned** all the new words by next time.
- Pham Xuan An **is said to have been** a perfect spy.
- America **is thought to have been discovered** by Columbus before others.

## Notes & exceptions

- `was / were to have + PII` always implies that the plan failed: *The road was to have been upgraded* means it was not.
- The passive perfect infinitive is `to have been + PII`.

- [[Continuous Infinitive]] — action in progress at the moment spoken about
- [[Perfect Continuous Infinitive]] — earlier action seen as ongoing
