# Phrasal Verbs With Hand Hang And Hold

> [!abstract] In one line
> Three H-verbs that build idioms about giving things to people, waiting, and holding something back.

## Examples — hand

| Phrasal verb | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| hand down to = pass on to | transmit to a later generation | The farm was **handed down to** his eldest son. |
| hand in | submit, give to a person in authority | All essays must be **handed in** on Friday. |
| hand back | return, give back | The teacher **handed back** our tests. |
| hand over | transfer power or possession | The outgoing minister **handed over** to his successor. |
| hand out = give out | distribute | Volunteers **handed out** leaflets at the station. |

## Examples — hang

| Phrasal verb | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| hang around / hang round | loiter, wait about doing nothing | Don't **hang around** outside the school gates. |
| hang on = hold on | wait a moment (especially on the phone) | **Hang on** — I'll see if she is in. |
| hang up | end a telephone call | She **hung up** before I could explain. |
| hang out | hang washing outside; spend time somewhere | I **hung out** the washing before it rained. |

## Examples — hold

| Phrasal verb | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| hold on | wait | **Hold on** a second, I'm nearly ready. |
| hold off = put off | delay, postpone | They **held off** the decision until Monday. |
| hold back | restrain, keep under control | She could hardly **hold back** her tears. |
| hold up | delay, obstruct; rob | We were **held up** by roadworks for an hour. |

## Notes & exceptions

- `hang on` and `hold on` are interchangeable when they mean *wait*, but only `hang up` ends a call.
- `hang` is irregular in these idioms (*hang – hung – hung*); the regular form *hanged* is used only for execution.
- `hold up` in the sense of *rob* is normally passive or has a noun form: *a hold-up*.
- `hand in`, `hand out` and `hand back` are separable: *hand **it** in* → see [[Separable Phrasal Verbs]].

## Common mistakes

- ❌ She hanged up the phone. → ✅ She **hung up** the phone.
- ❌ Please hold on the line for a moment. → ✅ Please **hold on**, I'll put you through.
