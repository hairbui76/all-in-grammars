# Possessive Adjectives

> [!abstract] In one line
> Words placed before a noun to show who the noun belongs to.

## The set

| Personal pronoun | Possessive adjective |
|---|---|
| I | my |
| you | your |
| he | his |
| she | her |
| it | its |
| we | our |
| you | your |
| they | their |

## Rules

- A possessive adjective ALWAYS has a noun after it.
- It never changes for the number or gender of the noun that follows; it agrees with the OWNER.

## Examples

- **My** father is a doctor.
- This is **her** book. / These are **her** books.
- Hanoi is famous for **its** beauty-spots and for **its** glorious historical victories.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ its' / it's for possession → ✅ `its` (no apostrophe); `it's` = *it is*.
- ❌ her books are hers books → ✅ **her** books.
