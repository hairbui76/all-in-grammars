# Possessive Pronouns

> [!abstract] In one line
> A possessive pronoun replaces "possessive adjective + noun" so the noun does not have to be repeated.

## Form

| Possessive adjective | Possessive pronoun |
|---|---|
| my | mine |
| your | yours |
| his | his |
| her | hers |
| its | its |
| our | ours |
| your | yours |
| their | theirs |

`It is my car.` ⇔ `It is mine.`

## When to use

- As the subject of a verb
- As the object of a verb
- Standing alone, with no noun after it

## Examples

- Your shirt is red. **Mine** is white.
- Her father is an engineer. **His** is a policeman.
- **Ours** is a developing country.
- This book is **mine** / **his** / **theirs** / Peter's.
- These pens are **hers**.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ This book is mine book → ✅ This book is **mine** / This is **my** book.
- ❌ your's, her's → ✅ `yours`, `hers` (no apostrophe).
