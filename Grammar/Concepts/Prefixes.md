# Prefixes

> [!abstract] In one line
> A prefix is added in front of a root word to make a new word; it is not a word on its own and has no meaning in isolation.

## Form

`prefix + root word` → new word, usually of the same word class

## Prefixes of degree, size and quantity

| Prefix | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **over-** | too much | crowded → over-crowded |
| **under-** | too little, below | paid → under-paid |
| **sub-** | under, below | way → subway |
| **bi-** | two | lingual → bi-lingual |
| **semi-** | half | final → semi-final |
| **out-** | beyond, more than | grow → outgrow |
| **multi-** | many | racial → multi-racial |
| **super-** | above, extra large | market → supermarket |
| **inter-** | between, among | national → international |

- There are many **over-crowded** cities in the world.
- The workers are against the management because they're **under-paid**.
- That road is very dangerous. Use the **subway** to get across.
- We should have a **bi-lingual** dictionary to learn English better.
- Korea reached the **semi-final** of the World Cup in 2002.
- Children quickly **outgrow** their shoes.
- There were a lot of **international** sports competitions in 2000.

## Prefixes of time, attitude and effect

| Prefix | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **fore-** | before, in advance | see → foresee |
| **mid-** | in the middle of | autumn → mid-autumn |
| **post-** | after | graduate → postgraduate |
| **pre-** | before | school → pre-school |
| **re-** | again | write → rewrite |
| **ex-** | former | coach → ex-coach |
| **anti-** | against | war → anti-war |
| **pro-** | in favour of | Vietnamese → pro-Vietnamese |
| **non-** | not, absence of | existence → non-existence |
| **co-** | together with | operate → co-operate |
| **en-** | to make, to cause | danger → endanger |

- Do you **foresee** any problems with the new system?
- Children often get moon cakes in **mid-autumn** festivals.
- There are nursery schools for **pre-school** children.
- You have to **rewrite** your composition. It's full of mistakes.
- We should **co-operate** for a better future of the world.
- He likes Vietnamese people and culture. He's very **pro-Vietnamese**.

## Notes & exceptions

- Prefixes never carry the primary stress and never change it → see [[Prefixes and Stress]].
- Negative prefixes form their own family → see [[Negative Prefixes]].
