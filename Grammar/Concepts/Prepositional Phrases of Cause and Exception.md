# Prepositional Phrases of Cause and Exception

> [!abstract] In one line
> Two-word and three-word prepositions that introduce a cause, a substitution or an exception, and are always followed by a noun or `-ing`.

## Form

`complex preposition + noun / pronoun / V-ing`

They never introduce a clause with a subject and a verb — that is the job of a conjunction such as `because`.

## Examples — cause and source

| Phrase | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| according to | as stated by | **According to** the timetable, the train leaves at six. |
| due to | caused by, because of | The delay was **due to** bad weather. |
| due for | scheduled or expected to receive | She is **due for** promotion this year. |
| owing to | because of | **Owing to** the strike, no post was delivered. |
| on account of | because of | The match was cancelled **on account of** the rain. |
| because of | as a result of | We stayed at home **because of** the storm. |
| thanks to | as a happy result of | **Thanks to** your advice, I passed the exam. |

## Examples — substitution, reference and exception

| Phrase | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| instead of | in place of | Let's walk **instead of** taking a taxi. |
| in front of | directly before, ahead of | A tall man sat **in front of** me. |
| in terms of | with regard to, measured by | **In terms of** sales, the year was excellent. |
| in charge of | responsible for | Who is **in charge of** the department? |
| except for | apart from, not counting | The room was empty **except for** a chair. |
| apart from | except for; in addition to | **Apart from** the ending, I liked the film. |

## Notes & exceptions

- `because of` + noun, but `because` + clause: *because of the rain* / *because it rained* → see [[Because and Because Of]].
- `due to` traditionally follows the verb `be` (*The delay was due to fog*); `owing to` introduces the whole sentence more freely.
- A verb after any of these becomes a gerund: *instead of **going***, *in charge of **running** the office* → see [[Gerund After Prepositions]].
- `in spite of` and `despite` belong to the same family but express concession, not cause → see [[Despite and In Spite Of]].

## Common mistakes

- ❌ Because of it rained, we stayed in. → ✅ **Because of** the rain, we stayed in.
- ❌ Instead of go, he stayed. → ✅ **Instead of going**, he stayed.
