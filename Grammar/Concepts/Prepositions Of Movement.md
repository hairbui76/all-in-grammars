# Prepositions Of Movement

> [!abstract] In one line
> These prepositions describe the path a person or thing travels, not a fixed position.

## The list

| Preposition | Movement |
|---|---|
| `out of` | from inside to outside |
| `into` | from outside to inside |
| `up` | from a lower to a higher point |
| `down` | from a higher to a lower point |
| `along` | following the length of something |
| `across` | from one side to the other |
| `through` | entering and coming out the other side |
| `off` | away from a surface |
| `over` | above and to the other side |
| `on to` | up and onto a surface |

## Examples

- The man went **out of** the hotel.
- The elephants ran back **into** the jungle.
- The fireman climbed **up** the ladder and rescued the little boy.
- The man went quickly **down** the hill.
- There are many houses **along** the road to the village.
- The little boy ran **across** the street.
- We went **through** a wood to the cottage.
- The man jumped **off** the wall.
- The boys climbed **over** the wall into the orchard.
- The thief climbed **on to** the roof.

## Notes & exceptions

- `across` moves over a flat open surface; `through` moves inside something with volume (a wood, a tunnel, a crowd).
- Many of these words are also adverb particles in [[Phrasal Verbs]] (*climb up, jump off, go through*).
