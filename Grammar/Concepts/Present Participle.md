# Present Participle

> [!abstract] In one line
> The `-ing` form of a verb, used with an active meaning: as part of continuous tenses, as an adjective, or as the head of a participle clause.

## Form

```
V-ing
```

Examples of the form: `feeling, writing, lying, getting, forgetting, picnicking`.
Spelling details are collected in [[Spelling Rules for -ing Forms]].

## Main uses

1. **In all continuous tenses** (present, past, future and their perfect continuous forms)
2. **Before a noun, as an adjective** with an active meaning
3. **After `have + object`** in the causative-style pattern `have + obj + V-ing`
4. **After verbs of perception** — see [[Verbs of Perception with V-ing and Bare Infinitive]]
5. **After certain verbs and expressions** — see [[Verb Patterns with V-ing]]
6. **As a reduced clause**, replacing a subject + active verb — see [[Participle Clauses]] and [[Reduced Relative Clauses]]

## Examples

- They **had been living** there for 5 years when the Civil War broke out.
- Come in! We **have been waiting** for you for a long time.
- By the end of next year I **will have been teaching** English for 12 years.
- a **sleeping** child, a **lying** dog, **dripping** water, a **laughing** girl, a **floating** house
- He had me **swimming** for a week.
- I won't have him **cleaning** his bicycle in the kitchen.

## Notes & exceptions

- Reporting participles such as `adding that`, `warning sb that`, `reminding sb that`, `pointing out that` can introduce an indirect clause:
  - "Please don't take a risk," his girlfriend said. "I can't live without you." → She begged him not to take a risk, **adding that** she couldn't live without him.
  - "You'd better set off early. The roads will be crowded," she said. → She advised me to set off early, **warning me** that the roads would be crowded.

## Contrast with

- [[Past Participle]] — passive meaning (`boiled water` vs `boiling water`)
- [[Perfect Participle]] — `Having + V3`, for an action completed earlier
- [[Gerunds]] — same `-ing` form, but functioning as a noun
