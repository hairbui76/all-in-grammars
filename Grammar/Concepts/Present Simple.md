# Present Simple

> [!abstract] In one line
> States facts, habits and permanent situations that are true around the present time.

## Form

```
(+) S + V(-s/-es)          I/We/You/They/plural noun + V
                           He/She/It/singular noun + V(-s/-es)
(-) S + don't/doesn't + V(bare)
(?) Do/Does + S + V(bare)?
```

Add `-es` when the verb ends in `s, ss, sh, ch, x, z` (*passes, washes, watches, fixes*), in `o` (*do → does, go → goes*), or in consonant + `y` (change `y` to `i`: *study → studies*). Otherwise add `-s`.

## When to use

- Likes, dislikes and feelings in the present
- Habits and repeated daily actions
- Someone's ability or capacity in the present
- General truths and laws of nature
- In the `if`-clause of a real present condition (Type 1)
- After a time conjunction (`when, by the time, as soon as, until/till, after, before, while`) in a sentence about the future
- Timetabled events: transport schedules, films, TV programmes, fixed personal plans

## Examples

- I **like** classical music. — She **hates** getting up early.
- My mother usually **stays** up late and **gets** up early.
- He **speaks** English fluently.
- The earth **goes** round the sun. — Oil **floats** on water.
- If you **work** hard, you will pass the exam.
- When I **get** home, I will phone you. — As soon as the class **is** over, we will go home.
- The train to Ha Noi **leaves** Thai Nguyen station at 7 o'clock tomorrow morning.

## Notes & exceptions

- This tense is for STABLE situations, so it goes with stable time expressions: *every day, every week, every month, on Mondays, in the morning/afternoon/evening, at noon, in spring/summer/autumn/winter, every four years, every hour*.
- ❌ When I will get home, I will phone you → ✅ When I **get** home, ...

## Contrast with

- [[Present Continuous]] — temporary situations happening around now
- [[Present Perfect]] — a past action with present relevance
- [[Be Going To]] — plans already decided
