# Present Subjunctive

> [!abstract] In one line
> The present subjunctive has exactly the same form as the infinitive, so `be` stays `be` and no `-s` is added in the third person singular.

## Form

```
S + V(bare)
```

- `be` is `be` for all persons
- all other verbs look like the present tense, **except** that the third person singular takes **no `-s`**

## Contrast of forms

| Indicative | Subjunctive |
|---|---|
| The queen **lives** here. | Long **live** the queen! |
| He **is** prepared. | It is essential that he **be** prepared. |

## Fixed exclamations of wish or hope

Often involving supernatural powers:

- (God) **bless** you!
- **God save** the queen!
- **Heaven help** us!
- **Curse** this fog!
- **Come what may**, we'll stand by you!
- **If need be** we can always bring another car. (= if it is necessary)

## Notes & exceptions

- Beyond these fixed phrases, the present subjunctive survives mainly in `that`-clauses after verbs of advising and demanding — see [[Subjunctive in That Clauses]] — and after impersonal expressions — see [[Subjunctive with Impersonal Expressions]].
- In these clauses, `should` may be omitted, leaving the bare infinitive alone: `It is essential that he **be** prepared.`

## Common mistakes

- ❌ God saves the queen! → ✅ **God save** the queen!
- ❌ It is essential that he is prepared. → ✅ It is essential that he **be** prepared.
