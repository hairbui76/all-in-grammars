# Question Word Plus Infinitive

> [!abstract] In one line
> A question word plus a full infinitive forms a shortened noun clause after certain verbs.

## Form

`V + how / what / when / where / which / who + (N) + to + V`

`whether` is also possible; `why` is not used in this pattern.

## Verbs used with this structure

ask · decide · discover · find out · forget · know · learn · remember ·
see (= understand) · show + obj · think · understand · want to know · wonder

## Examples

- I don't know **what to say**.
- Have you decided **where to go** for your holiday?
- He discovered **how to open** the safe.
- I have found out **where to buy** fruit cheaply.
- I don't know **which button to press**.
- I haven't decided **when to buy** a house in TN city.
- Please show me **how to do** this.
- The man asked me **how to get** to the airport.

## Notes & exceptions

- The structure is a reduced noun clause: *I don't know what to say* = *I don't know what I should say*.
- With `show`, `tell` and `teach` an object usually comes before the question word: *I showed **him** how to use the camera.*
- A noun may follow the question word: *which **button** to press*, *what **time** to leave*.
