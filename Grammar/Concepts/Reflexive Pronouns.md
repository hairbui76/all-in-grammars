# Reflexive Pronouns

> [!abstract] In one line
> Used when the subject and the object of the verb are the same person or thing.

## Form

| Subject | Reflexive |
|---|---|
| I | myself |
| you | yourself |
| he | himself |
| she | herself |
| it | itself |
| we | ourselves |
| you | yourselves |
| they | themselves |

## When to use

- The action comes back onto the doer
- To emphasise that a person did something without help

## Examples

- I am teaching **myself** English.
- Jack and Tom blamed **themselves** for the accident.
- I hope you both enjoy **yourselves**.

## Notes & exceptions

- Singular forms end in `-self`, plural forms in `-selves`; `yourself` and `yourselves` are therefore distinguished, unlike the plain pronoun `you`.
- Some verbs that are reflexive in Vietnamese are not reflexive in English: *He washed* / *She dressed quickly* (not *washed himself* in ordinary use).

## Common mistakes

- ❌ themself, theirselves, hisself → ✅ **themselves**, **himself**.
