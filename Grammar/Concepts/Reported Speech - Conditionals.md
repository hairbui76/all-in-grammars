# Reported Speech - Conditionals

> [!abstract] In one line
> Only a type 1 conditional backshifts when reported; types 2 and 3 keep their verb forms and only the pronouns and time words change.

## Form

```
Type 1 quoted:  If + present, will + V    ->  if + past, would + V
Type 2 quoted:  If + past, would + V      ->  unchanged
Type 3 quoted:  If + past perfect, would have + V3  ->  unchanged
```

## When to use

- Apply the ordinary backshift rule to type 1 only
- Leave types 2 and 3 alone, because their past forms are already subjunctive and cannot go further back

## Examples

Type 1:

- "If the weather is fine, we will go camping next week," the boy said → The boy said that **if the weather was fine, he would go** camping the following week.
- "If you don't take it easy for a few days, you will have another heart attack," the doctor said → The doctor warned the patient that **if he didn't take** it easy for a few days, he **would have** another heart attack.

Type 2:

- "If I had a lot of money, I would buy a modern car," he said → He said that **if he had** a lot of money, he **would buy** a modern car.
- "What would you do if you were rich?" she asked → She asked me **what I would do if I were** rich.

Type 3:

- "If I had known that you were ill, I would have come to see you," said one of my friends → One of my friends said that **if he had known** that I was ill, he **would have come** to see me.

## Notes & exceptions

- A type 2 conditional beginning `If I were you, ...` or `If I were in your shoes / situation / position, ...` is normally reported with **advise**, not with an if-clause:
  - "If I were you, I would apply for the job," she said → She **advised me to apply** for the job.
  - "If I were in your shoes, I wouldn't turn down their invitation," Jack said → Jack **advised me not to turn down** their invitation.
  - "If I were you, I would be more interested in learning English," said my teacher → My teacher **advised me to be** more interested in learning English.
- Pattern: `advise sb to do smth` / `advise sb not to do smth` = `advise sb against doing smth`.
