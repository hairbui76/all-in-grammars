# Reporting Verbs

> [!abstract] In one line
> The reporting verb is chosen to match the function of the original words, and each verb brings its own pattern.

## Form

| Pattern | Verbs |
|---|---|
| `V + (that) + clause` | say, explain, admit, reply, agree, promise, warn |
| `V + O + (that) + clause` | tell, remind, warn, promise |
| `V + O + (not) + to + V` | tell, ask, remind, recommend, warn, invite, encourage, beg, plead with, implore, advise, order, request, urge, command, forbid |
| `V + to + V` | offer, promise, refuse, threaten |
| `V + (O) + for + V-ing` | thank, apologise, blame, congratulate |
| `V + O` | thank, curse, congratulate, call |

## When to use

- Do not always use `say`: choose the verb that names the speech act (order, warn, offer, beg, advise ...)
- The pattern follows from the verb, so learn verb and pattern together

## Examples

- "Shall I bring you some tea?" Tom asked → Tom **offered to bring** me some tea.
- "Don't forget to order the wine," said Mrs Pitt → Mrs Pitt **reminded** her husband **to order** the wine.
- "Thank you," he said → He **thanked** me.
- "Congratulations," he said → He **congratulated** me.
- He said to her, "I'm sorry for breaking your camera" → He **apologized to her for breaking / having broken** her camera.
- "I will never do this again," he said to her → He **promised** her **not to do** that again.
- "Curse this frog," she said → She **cursed** the frog.
- "Liar!" she said → She **called** me a liar.
- "Damn!" he said → He **swore**.
- "Stand still!" he said angrily → He **angrily asked** me **to stand** still.
- "May I see your passport, please?" said the customs officer → The customs officer **asked** me **to show** him my passport.

## Notes & exceptions

- `say` cannot take a personal object directly (`say to sb that ...`), while `tell` requires one (`tell sb that ...`).
- Greetings and good wishes take `wish`: *"Happy New Year," he said → He **wished** me a happy new year.*
- The choice of verb often makes the `that`-clause unnecessary, producing a much shorter report.
