# Separable Phrasal Verbs

> [!abstract] In one line
> When the particle is an adverb, the object may stand either after the particle or between the verb and the particle — and a pronoun object *must* stand in the middle.

## Form

`V + particle + object` = `V + object + particle`
`V + pronoun + particle` (obligatory word order)

In reference lists a separable verb is written with the object inside it:
`turn something off`, `bring someone up`, `blow something up`.

## When to use

- Use either order with a noun object: *Please turn off the light.* / *Please turn the light off.*
- Use only the middle position with a personal pronoun (`it, him, her, them, me, us`).
- Separation is normal when the object is short; keep the verb together when the object is a long phrase.

## Examples

- Please **turn off** all the lights as you leave the room.
- Please **put** the light **on**, it's getting dark.
- If you don't know the meaning of a word, **look** it **up** in a dictionary.
- The soldiers **blew up** the enemy bridge. / We have to **blow** 50 balloons **up** for the party.
- My grandparents **brought** me **up** after my parents died.
- Our teacher **broke** the final project **down** into three separate parts.

## Notes & exceptions

- Common separable verbs: `turn on/off`, `put on/off`, `pick up`, `look up`, `take off`, `bring up`, `blow up`, `back up`, `break down`, `cut off`, `fill in`, `give up`, `hand out`, `knock down`, `leave out`, `let down`, `make up`, `pay back`, `point out`, `put off`, `send back`, `sort out`, `take up`, `throw away`, `try on`, `turn down`, `wake up`, `write down`.
- The same string may be separable in one meaning and not in another: `break something down` (divide into parts, separable) vs `break down` (stop functioning, intransitive).
- Intransitive phrasal verbs (`break out`, `come about`, `show up`, `pass out`) take no object at all and so never separate.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ Turn off it. → ✅ **Turn it off.**
- ❌ I picked up her at the station. → ✅ I **picked her up** at the station.
