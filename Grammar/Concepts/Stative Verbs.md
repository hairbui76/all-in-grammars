# Stative Verbs

> [!abstract] In one line
> Verbs describing states rather than actions; they are not normally used in any continuous tense.

## The list

`want · need · belong · suppose · consist · contain · know · like · mean · realise · remember · prefer · mind · depend · hate · seem · believe · think (= believe) · have (= possess) · sound · taste · smell · be`

## Examples

- I **need** a car now.
- I **am** very hungry at the moment.
- This soup **tastes** very salty.
- I **have** two brothers. (= possess)

## Notes & exceptions

- With these verbs, use the simple tense in place of the continuous one: [[Present Simple]] instead of [[Present Continuous]], [[Present Perfect]] instead of [[Present Perfect Continuous]], [[Past Perfect]] instead of [[Past Perfect Continuous]].
- Some of them have a second, dynamic meaning which CAN be continuous: *I'm thinking about your offer* (= considering), *He is having dinner* (= eating), *She is tasting the soup* (= testing it).
- `always` is not used with the present perfect continuous; use the present perfect instead: *I have always loved you.*

## Common mistakes

- ❌ I am knowing the answer → ✅ I **know** the answer.
- ❌ I am wanting a coffee → ✅ I **want** a coffee.
