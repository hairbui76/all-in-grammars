# Subjunctive with Should

> [!abstract] In one line
> After certain verbs and adjectives of demand, advice or importance, the `that`-clause uses `should + bare infinitive`, and `should` may be dropped.

## Form

- `S + V + that + S + (should) + V(bare)`
- `It is/was + adjective + that + S + (should) + V(bare)`

Verbs taking this pattern:

`advise` · `agree` · `arrange` · `ask` · `beg` · `command` · `decide` · `demand` ·
`determine` · `insist` · `order` · `propose` · `recommend` · `request` · `stipulate` ·
`suggest` · `urge` · `prefer` · `decree`

Also with `be anxious` and `be determined`.

Adjectives taking `that ... should`:

`advisable` · `better` · `desirable` · `essential` · `imperative` · `important` ·
`natural` · `necessary` · `crucial` · `vital`

## Examples

- He insisted that his son **(should) be named** after his grandfather.
- They have decided to sell the house. → They have decided that the house **(should) be sold**.
- They suggested making the tests easier. → They suggested that the tests **should be made** easier.
- She advised keeping the gate locked. → She advised that the gate **should be kept** locked.
- He suggested that a petition **(should) be drawn** up.
- It is essential that rare animals **(should) be protected**.
- It is advisable that everyone **have** a map.
- It is essential for him **to be prepared** for this. / It is essential that he **(should) be prepared** for this.
- It is only right that she **(should) have** a share.

## Notes & exceptions

- When `should` is omitted the verb stays in the bare form for all persons — `that everyone have a map`, not `has`.
- The `that`-clause is very often passive, which is why this pattern is taught together with the passive voice.
- `It is + adjective + for sb + to V` is an alternative to the `that ... should` clause.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ It is essential that everyone has a map. → ✅ It is essential that everyone **have** a map.
