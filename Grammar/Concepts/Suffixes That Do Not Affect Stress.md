# Suffixes That Do Not Affect Stress

> [!abstract] In one line
> Apart from the stress-carrying and stress-shifting endings, every other suffix is neutral — you ignore it and stress the root word as it stands.

## Rule

`root + neutral suffix` → keep the stress the root would have on its own

## How to use a neutral suffix

1. Strip the neutral suffix off.
2. If another, non-neutral suffix is now exposed, apply its rule.
3. If not, decide the stress from the vowel sounds of the root.

- `entertainment` → drop `-ment`, `-ain` is stress-carrying → third syllable.
- `historical` → drop `-al`, `-ic` shifts stress to the syllable before it → second syllable.
- `friendliness` → drop `-ness` and `-ly` → stress the root `friend` → first syllable.
