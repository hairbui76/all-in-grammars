# Suffixes That Shift Stress Three Syllables Back

> [!abstract] In one line
> With these endings you count three syllables back from the end of the word, and that syllable is stressed.

## Rule

`STRESSED + σ + σ` counted from the end of the word (the third syllable from the right)

## The list

| Suffix | Examples with transcription |
|---|---|
| **-y** | biology /baɪˈɒlədʒi/, accompany /əˈkʌmpəni/ |
| **-ce** | independence /ˌɪndɪˈpendəns/, difference /ˈdɪfrəns/ |
| **-ate** | graduate /ˈɡrædʒueɪt/, concentrate /ˈkɒnsəntreɪt/ |
| **-ise / -ize** | apologise /əˈpɒlədʒaɪz/ |
| **-ism / -izm** | tourism /ˈtʊərɪzm/, criticism /ˈkrɪtɪsɪzm/ |

## Notes & exceptions

- Words ending in **-ary** count **four** syllables back: dictionary /ˈdɪkʃənəri/, vocabulary /vəˈkæbjʊləri/, secretary /ˈsekrətəri/ — but documentary /ˌdɒkjʊˈmentəri/ is irregular.
- `obligatory` /əˈblɪɡətəri/ breaks the `-y` rule and is stressed on the second syllable.
