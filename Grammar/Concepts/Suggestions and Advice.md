# Suggestions and Advice

> [!abstract] In one line
> Proposing an action for us to do together (a suggestion) or telling someone what would be good for them (advice).

## Useful patterns

### Suggesting

| Expression | Following form | Typical response |
|---|---|---|
| `Shall we ...?` | bare infinitive | `OK. Where shall we go?` |
| `Let's ...` | bare infinitive | `Great. Let's go.` |
| `Why don't we ...?` | bare infinitive | `That's a good idea.` |
| `How about ...?` / `What about ...?` | noun or `V-ing` | `That would be nice.` |
| `Do you have any plans tonight?` | — | `Yes, I have a date.` |

### Advising

| Expression | Typical response |
|---|---|
| `You should + V` | `Yes, maybe I should.` |
| `Maybe you should + V` | `You're right.` |
| `You'd better + V` | `I suppose so.` |
| `If I were you, I would + V` | `That's a good idea.` |
| `Maybe you can + V` | `I don't think so. I'm teaching all summer.` |

## When to use

- `Shall we ...?`, `Let's ...` and `Why don't we ...?` include the speaker; `You should ...` does not.
- `How about` and `What about` take a noun or `-ing`, never a bare infinitive.
- Soften advice with `Maybe` or `I think` when the other person has not asked for it.

## Examples

- "I have a terrible headache." — "**Maybe you should take a rest.**"
- "**Why don't we go** to the show?" — "That's a good idea."
- "**How about an evening riverboat tour?**" — "That would be nice."
- "Maybe you can take a vacation next month." — "I don't think so. I'm teaching all summer."

## Common mistakes

- ❌ How about to go on a riverboat tour? → ✅ How about **going** on a riverboat tour?
- ❌ Let's to go now. → ✅ **Let's go** now.
