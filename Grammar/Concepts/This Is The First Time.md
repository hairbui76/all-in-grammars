# This Is The First Time

> [!abstract] In one line
> After `This is the first/second/third ... time`, the verb that follows goes into the present perfect.

## Form

`This is the first / second / third ... time + S + have/has + past participle`

## Examples

- This is the first time I **have travelled** by plane.
- This is the second time you **have broken** a bowl. You broke one yesterday.
- This is the fifth time I **have studied** English here.
- This is the first time I **have eaten** this kind of food.

## Notes & exceptions

Three sentences say the same thing and are freely rewritten into one another:

1. This is the first time I **have worn** this shirt.
2. I **have never worn** this shirt before.
3. I **am wearing** this shirt for the first time.

## Contrast with

- [[Present Perfect]] — the tense required in the second clause
- [[Present Continuous]] — used in the third paraphrase above
