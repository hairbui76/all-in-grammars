# Verb Patterns with V-ing

> [!abstract] In one line
> A group of common verbs is regularly followed by an `-ing` form, either directly or after an object.

## Patterns

| Pattern | Verbs | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `have + obj + V-ing` | have | He had me **swimming** for a week. |
| `catch / find / leave + sb + V-ing` | catch, find, leave | I caught her **picking** apples. |
| `spend + money/time + V-ing / on + N` | spend | He spent the afternoon **repairing** the car. |
| `waste + money/time + V-ing / on + N` | waste | Don't waste money **buying** things you don't need. |
| `be busy + V-ing / with + N` | be busy | She is busy **doing** her housework. |
| `go / come + V-ing` | go, come | They come **dancing** every Saturday evening. |

## Examples

- He had me **swimming** for a week.
- I won't have him **cleaning** his bicycle in the kitchen.
- If she catches you **reading** her diary, she will be furious.
- I caught her **picking** apples in the orchard.
- I found him **fishing** in the river.
- I left them **talking** to each other for a long time.
- She is busy **doing** her housework.
- Women spend a lot of money **on cosmetics**.
- Don't waste money **buying** things you don't need.
- He wasted the whole afternoon **repairing** the car.
- They come **dancing** every Saturday evening.
- How often do you **go shopping**?
- As a child I used to **go swimming**.

## Notes & exceptions

- `go / come + V-ing` is used mainly with physical activities: `go shopping, skiing, sightseeing, sailing, camping, fishing, hunting, picnicking, swimming`.
- `spend / waste` take either `+ V-ing` or `+ on + noun`:
  - `Spend + a sum of money / a period of time + V-ing` or `+ on + N`
  - `Be busy with + N` or `Be busy + V-ing`

## Common mistakes

- ❌ She is busy to do her housework. → ✅ She is busy **doing** her housework.
- ❌ He spent the afternoon to repair the car. → ✅ He spent the afternoon **repairing** the car.
- ❌ I go to swim every Sunday. → ✅ I **go swimming** every Sunday.
