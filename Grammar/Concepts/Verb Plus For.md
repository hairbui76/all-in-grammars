# Verb Plus For

> [!abstract] In one line
> `For` follows verbs of wanting, seeking, paying and blaming, and marks the reason or the person who benefits.

## Form

`V + for + noun / V-ing`
`V + object + for + noun`

## Verb + for

| Combination | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| apply **for** sth | make an application for | She **applied for** the scholarship. |
| ask **for** | request | He **asked for** more time. |
| arrange **for** | make plans so that | I **arranged for** a taxi to collect us. |
| beg **for** sth | plead for | The dog **begged for** food. |
| hope **for** | wish to get | We are **hoping for** better weather. |
| long **for** | want very much | She **longs for** the holidays. |
| look **for** | try to find | I am **looking for** my keys. |
| wait **for** | stay until sb or sth comes | Don't **wait for** me. |
| pay **for** sth | give money in exchange for | He **paid for** the tickets. |
| stand **for** | represent, symbolise | UN **stands for** United Nations. |
| work **for** sb | be employed by | She **works for** a bank. |
| care **for** | look after, like | Who **cares for** the garden? |

## Verb + object + for

| Combination | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| blame sb **for** sth | say sb is at fault for | Don't **blame** me **for** the mistake. |
| punish sb **for** sth | impose a penalty because of | He was **punished for** cheating. |
| fine sb **for** sth | make sb pay a penalty for | She was **fined for** speeding. |
| borrow sth **for** sb | take sth on loan on sb's behalf | I **borrowed** a pen **for** her. |
| provide sth **for** sb | make sth available to | The school **provides** lunch **for** pupils. |
| supply sth **for** sb | deliver sth to | They **supply** materials **for** builders. |
| fight **with** sb **for** sth | struggle against sb over sth | They **fought with** the council **for** funding. |

## Fixed verb + noun + for

| Phrase | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| make room **for** | create space for | Please **make room for** the trolley. |
| make allowance **for** | take into account, be lenient about | **Make allowance for** his inexperience. |

## Notes & exceptions

- `provide / supply` take either `sth **for** sb` or `sb **with** sth` → see [[Verb Plus With And From]].
- `apply for` a job, but `apply to` a person or organisation → see [[Verb Plus To]].
- `pay for sth` but `pay sb`: the preposition disappears before a person.
- A verb after `for` takes `-ing`: `apologize **for** being late` → see [[Gerund After Prepositions]].

## Common mistakes

- ❌ I am waiting you. → ✅ I am **waiting for** you.
- ❌ He blamed me about the accident. → ✅ He **blamed** me **for** the accident.

- [[Verb Plus Of]] — cause and content
