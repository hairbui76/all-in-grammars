# Verb Plus Object Plus Infinitive

> [!abstract] In one line
> Many verbs of influencing, ordering and permitting take an object followed by the full infinitive.

## Form

`S + V + object + to + V`

Negative: `S + V + object + not + to + V`

## The list

advise · allow · beg · bribe · change · command · compel · encourage · entitle ·
forbid · force · implore · induce · instruct · invite · oblige · order · permit ·
persuade · plead with · recommend · remind · request · show (how) · teach (how) ·
tell (how) · tempt · urge · warn

## Examples

- I **persuaded him to change** his mind.
- I **warned my students not to play** truant.
- My friends **advised me to go on** studying at the Teachers' Training College.
- She **induced me to steal** the money.
- I **showed him how to use** the camera.
- My mother **urged me to leave** early.
- She **begged me to do** her favour.
- The robber **forced everybody to lie** on the floor.

## Notes & exceptions

- The negative particle `not` goes immediately before `to`: *Mr Brown told the schoolgirls **not to eat** in class.*
- `make`, `let` and `have` take a **bare** infinitive in this pattern, not `to + V` → see [[Bare Infinitive]] and [[Causative Form]].
- The passive of this pattern keeps the full infinitive: *The tourists were encouraged to visit the museum.*

## Contrast with

- [[Bare Infinitive]] — `make / let / have + object + V(bare)`
- [[Causative Form]] — `have / get something done`
