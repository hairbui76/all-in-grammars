# Verb Plus On And In

> [!abstract] In one line
> `On` follows verbs of reliance, insistence and focus; `in` follows verbs of participation, belief and achievement.

## Verb + on

`V + on + noun / V-ing` · `V + object + on + noun`

| Combination | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| depend **on** / rely **on** | count on, be determined by | We **depend on** the bus service. |
| count **on** | trust to be there | You can **count on** me. |
| insist **on** sth / V-ing | firmly demand | He **insisted on** paying the bill. |
| persist **on** / **in** | keep doing stubbornly | She **persisted in** asking questions. |
| concentrate **on** | focus attention on | **Concentrate on** your driving. |
| base sth **on** sth | build it from | The film is **based on** a true story. |
| live **on** sth | survive using | They **live on** a small pension. |
| congratulate sb **on** sth | offer good wishes for | I **congratulated** her **on** the award. |
| play a trick **on** sb | deceive sb for fun | The boys **played a trick on** their teacher. |

## Verb + in

`V + in + noun / V-ing`

| Combination | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| believe **in** | trust the value or existence of | She **believes in** hard work. |
| succeed **in** sth / V-ing | manage to do | He **succeeded in** passing the exam. |
| participate **in** | take part in | Everyone **participated in** the clean-up. |
| take part **in** | join an activity | She **took part in** the debate. |
| join **in** | start taking part with others | Please **join in** the discussion. |
| be absorbed **in** | be deeply engaged in | He was **absorbed in** his work. |
| result **in** | have as its outcome | The storm **resulted in** long delays. |

## Arrive at and arrive in

| Pattern | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| arrive **at** | small places: a station, an airport, a bus stop | We **arrived at** the airport at six. |
| arrive **in** | cities and countries | They **arrived in** London on Friday. |

## Notes & exceptions

- Never say `arrive to`; use `at` for a point and `in` for a large area → see [[Prepositions Of Place]].
- `succeed **in** doing sth` uses `-ing`, never an infinitive → see [[Gerund After Prepositions]].
- `depend on` and `rely on` are interchangeable; both take `sb **for** sth`: `I depend on you for advice.`
- `insist on`, `persist in` and `concentrate on` are all followed by `V-ing`.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ We arrived to Paris. → ✅ We **arrived in** Paris.
- ❌ He succeeded to find a job. → ✅ He **succeeded in finding** a job.
