# Verb Suffixes

> [!abstract] In one line
> The ending `-en` is added to certain adjectives to make a verb meaning *to make / become more ...*.

## Form

`adjective + -en` → verb meaning *to make (more) + adjective*

| Adjective | Verb | Adjective | Verb |
|---|---|---|---|
| tight | tighten | less | lessen |
| weak | weaken | sharp | sharpen |
| light | lighten | wide | widen |

## Examples

- That screw is loose. **Tighten** it with a screwdriver.
- You can **lessen** the risk of theft by locking your bicycle.
- Do you have a knife? I want to **sharpen** my pencil.
- This road is very narrow but they're planning to **widen** it.

## Notes & exceptions

- The prefix `en-` does the same job from the front: `danger → endanger`, `rich → enrich` → see [[Prefixes]].
- `-ise / -ize` also builds verbs (`apology → apologise`, `modern → modernise`) and shifts stress three syllables from the end → see [[Suffixes That Shift Stress Three Syllables Back]].
- `-en` is stress-neutral: `ˈtighten`, `ˈwiden`.
