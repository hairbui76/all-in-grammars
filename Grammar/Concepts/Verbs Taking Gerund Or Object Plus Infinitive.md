# Verbs Taking Gerund Or Object Plus Infinitive

> [!abstract] In one line
> `advise, allow, recommend, encourage, permit, forbid` take a gerund when there is no personal object, but an object + `to`-infinitive when a person is named.

## Form

```
advise / allow / recommend / encourage / permit / forbid + V-ing
advise / allow / recommend / encourage / permit / forbid + Obj + to + V
```

## Examples

- She doesn't allow **smoking** in her room.
- She doesn't allow anybody **to smoke** in her room.
- He advised **taking** this route.
- He advised us **to take** this route.
- We were advised **to take** this route.

## Notes & exceptions

- In the passive, only the `to`-infinitive pattern is possible: *We were advised to take this route.*
- The meaning does not change; only the presence of a personal object decides the pattern.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ She doesn't allow anybody smoking in her room. → ✅ She doesn't allow anybody **to smoke** in her room.
- ❌ He advised us taking this route. → ✅ He advised us **to take** this route.
