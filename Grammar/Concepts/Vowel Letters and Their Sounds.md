# Vowel Letters and Their Sounds

> [!abstract] In one line
> Each vowel letter and vowel digraph maps onto a small set of predictable sounds — the basis of "odd one out" pronunciation questions.

## Single vowel letters

| Letter | Usual sounds | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `a` | `/æ/` · `/eɪ/` · `/ə/` | m**a**p, n**a**tional · t**a**ke, n**a**ture · terrible, principle |
| `e` | `/e/` · `/ɪ/` · `/ə/` · `/iː/` | **e**ducate, fl**e**xible · **e**xplore, r**e**place · f**e**ver, g**e**ne |
| `i` | `/ɪ/` · `/aɪ/` · `/ʌ/` · `/aʊ/` · `/ə/` · `/wʌ/` | f**i**t, sl**i**ppery · str**i**ve, m**i**ce · s**o**n, d**o**ne |
| `o` | `/ɒ/` · `/ɔː/` · `/əʊ/` · `/ʌ/` · `/ə/` | b**o**ttle, fl**o**ppy · b**o**ring, l**o**rd · g**o**, p**o**st · s**o**n, d**o**ne · purp**o**se |
| `u` | `/ʊ/` · `/ʌ/` · `/juː/` · `/uː/` · `/ə/` · `/ɜː/` | p**u**t, p**u**sh · m**u**ch, c**u**t · comp**u**ter, h**u**man · incl**u**de · s**u**ccess · b**u**rn |
| `y` | `/ɪ/` · `/aɪ/` | ph**y**sics, m**y**th · cit**y**, worr**y**, sh**y**, multipl**y** |

## Notes & exceptions

- These are tendencies, not laws; every new word should be learnt with its own transcription.
- The same spelling changes sound with word class: *invite* `/ɪnˈvaɪt/` (v) but *invitation* `/ˌɪnvɪˈteɪʃn/` (n); *nature* `/ˈneɪtʃə(r)/` (n) but *natural* `/ˈnætʃrəl/` (adj).
