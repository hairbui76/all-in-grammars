# English Grammar MOC

> [!abstract] In one line
> The root of this vault: 295 atomic grammar notes extracted from *Tập 26 Chuyên đề Ngữ pháp*, organised into 25 topic hubs.

## How to use this vault

1. Open **Graph view** (`Ctrl/Cmd + G`). Every note links to its hub, so the graph shows 25 clusters around this note.
2. Pick a hub below and read its notes in order. Each note is one rule: form, when to use, examples, common mistakes.
3. Follow the `## Contrast with` links — they connect the structures learners actually confuse. That is where most exam mistakes live.
4. Use `Ctrl/Cmd + O` to jump to any concept by name, and the backlinks pane to see what depends on it.

> [!tip] Graph view settings
> Set **Group** by the search `tag:#moc` and give it a colour — the hubs then stand out as the backbone of the graph.
