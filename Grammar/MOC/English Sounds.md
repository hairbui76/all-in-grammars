# English Sounds

> [!abstract] In one line
> Vowels, consonants and diphthongs, with their IPA symbols.

## Notes in this area

- [[Consonant Letters and Their Sounds]] — Several consonant letters and letter groups have more than one pronunciation, usually decided by the letter th
- [[English Consonant Sounds]] — English has 24 consonant sounds; several of them do not exist in Vietnamese and have to be learnt separately
- [[English Diphthongs and Triphthongs]] — A diphthong glides from one vowel to another inside a single syllable; a triphthong glides through three
- [[English Vowel Sounds]] — English has 20 vowel sounds; twelve of them are single (pure) vowels written with one IPA symbol
- [[Pronunciation of -ED Endings]] — The regular past ending `-ed` is read `/ɪd/`, `/t/` or `/d/`, decided by the final sound of the base verb
- [[Pronunciation of Final -S and -ES]] — The `-s/-es` ending on plural nouns and third-person verbs is read three different ways, decided by the sound 
- [[Stress and Vowel Sounds]] — If you can see the transcription of a word, the vowel qualities themselves tell you where the stress falls
- [[Syllables and Stress Marking]] — Every English word of two or more syllables has one syllable pronounced louder, higher and longer than the res
- [[Vowel Letters and Their Sounds]] — Each vowel letter and vowel digraph maps onto a small set of predictable sounds — the basis of "odd one out" p
