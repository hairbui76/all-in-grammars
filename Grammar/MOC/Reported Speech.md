# Reported Speech

> [!abstract] In one line
> Reporting what someone said, with the tense, pronoun and time shifts it forces.

## Notes in this area

- [[Backshift of Tenses]] — When the reporting verb is in the past, the verb of the quoted words moves one step further back in time
- [[Future Simple]] — `will + bare infinitive` — a decision, prediction, promise or offer made about the future
- [[Modal Changes in Reported Speech]] — Only a few modals change when speech is reported; those that are already past forms, and those expressing advi
- [[Past Perfect]] — The "past before the past": an action finished before another point or action in the past
- [[Past Perfect Continuous]] — An action that had been going on for a period of time up to a moment in the past
- [[Past Subjunctive in Reported Speech]] — Structures built on the unreal past — `wish`, `as if / as though`, `would rather`, `it's time`, `had better` —
- [[Pronoun Changes in Reported Speech]] — Personal, possessive and demonstrative pronouns are re-pointed from the original speaker's viewpoint to the re
- [[Reported Speech - Commands]] — Commands, requests, advice and invitations are reported with a reporting verb + object + `(not) to` + infiniti
- [[Reported Speech - Conditionals]] — Only a type 1 conditional backshifts when reported; types 2 and 3 keep their verb forms and only the pronouns 
- [[Reported Speech - Exclamations]] — An exclamation is reported either with `exclaim / say that` and normal word order, or with a verb naming the e
- [[Reported Speech - Questions]] — A reported question loses its question word order and its question mark; the tense, pronoun and time changes a
- [[Reported Speech - Statements]] — A statement is reported with `say/tell (that) + clause`, keeping the meaning but changing the words to the rep
- [[Reported Speech - Suggestions]] — Sentences that propose an action are reported with `suggest` (four possible patterns) or with `advise`, never 
- [[Reporting Verbs]] — The reporting verb is chosen to match the function of the original words, and each verb brings its own pattern
- [[Time and Place Changes in Reported Speech]] — Words that point to the speaker's "here and now" have to be re-anchored to the moment of reporting
